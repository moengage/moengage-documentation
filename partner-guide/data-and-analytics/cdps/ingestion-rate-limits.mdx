When you forward data into MoEngage from a Customer Data Platform (CDP) partner such as Segment, Adobe Real-Time CDP, or mParticle, inbound ingestion is subject to the rate limits and payload caps below.

## Ingestion Limits

Ingestion rate limits are enforced per workspace and vary by partner:

| Partner | Ingestion rate limit | On breach |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Twilio Segment | 500 RPS | Requests above the limit receive HTTP 429. Segment retries automatically, so data is not lost in most cases. |
| Adobe Real-Time CDP | 500 RPS | Requests above the limit receive HTTP 429. Adobe retries automatically. |
| mParticle | 15,000 RPM (~250 RPS) | Events above the threshold may be dropped and are not retried. |

The maximum payload size per request is **128 KB** across all partners. Requests larger than this receive a 413 error.

<Note>
  If your ingestion volume regularly approaches these limits, contact your MoEngage Customer Success Manager (CSM) to discuss your requirements.
</Note>
