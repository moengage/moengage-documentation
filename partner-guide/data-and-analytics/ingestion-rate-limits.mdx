Data imported into MoEngage from a partner is subject to an ingestion rate limit and a payload cap. The limits below are grouped by import type, because a partner sending users and events is limited differently from the same partner syncing cohorts.

<Note>
  These limits are valid unless a different value is explicitly called out in the documentation for a specific partner. Check the partner's own integration guide for any override.
</Note>

## CDP Import Limits

Applies to users and events forwarded from a Customer Data Platform, such as mParticle, Twilio Segment, and Adobe Real-Time CDP.

| Entity | Ingestion rate limit | Payload size | On breach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Users | 500 RPS | 128 KB | Requests above the limit receive a `429 (Too Many Requests)` error from MoEngage. Partners are expected to handle retries automatically. |
| Events | 500 RPS | 128 KB | Requests above the limit receive a `429 (Too Many Requests)` error from MoEngage. Partners are expected to handle retries automatically. |

## Cohort Sync Import Limits

Applies to cohorts and audiences synced into MoEngage. The rate limit depends on the partner.

| Partners | Entity | Ingestion rate limit | Payload size | On breach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mParticle, Amplitude, Hightouch | Cohort | 300 RPM | 128 KB | Requests above the limit receive a `429 (Too Many Requests)` error from MoEngage. Partners are expected to handle retries automatically. |
| Twilio Segment, Adobe Real-Time CDP | Cohort | 500 RPS | 128 KB | Requests above the limit receive a `429 (Too Many Requests)` error from MoEngage. Partners are expected to handle retries automatically. |

## Other Partner Import Limits

Applies to connected sources and other partner integrations, such as Ventrata, RevenueCat, Zoho CRM, and HubSpot CRM.

| Entity | Ingestion rate limit | Payload size | On breach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Users, Events, and Cohorts | 500 RPS | 128 KB | Requests above the limit receive a `429 (Too Many Requests)` error from MoEngage. Partners are expected to handle retries automatically. |

## File Import Limits

Applies to imports from S3, SFTP, and manual uploads.

| Entity | Hourly limit | Daily limit | On breach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Users | 600K | 14M | Files are queued until the limits reset. |
| Events | 5M | 40M | Files are queued until the limits reset. |

## Data Warehouse Import Limits

Applies to imports from Snowflake, Databricks, and BigQuery. These limits are shared across all import types, so users and events draw from the same pool.

| Entity | Hourly limit | Daily limit | On breach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Users and Events | 10M | 50M | Processing happens on a best-effort basis. |

<Note>
  If your ingestion volume regularly approaches any of these limits, contact your MoEngage Customer Success Manager (CSM) to discuss your requirements.
</Note>
