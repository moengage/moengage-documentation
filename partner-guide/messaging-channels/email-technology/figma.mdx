# Introduction

[Figma](https://www.figma.com/) is a collaborative design tool that design teams use to build email layouts. The MoEngage Figma plugin converts a Figma design into a production-ready HTML email template inside MoEngage.

You connect Figma from the App Marketplace in your MoEngage workspace and authorize MoEngage to access your Figma account. When you paste a Figma link into an email campaign and convert it, MoEngage uses that authorization to retrieve the design from Figma and returns the HTML.

<Note>
  You install the plugin in your MoEngage workspace, not in Figma. MoEngage imports the design from Figma. Figma does not push the design to MoEngage.
</Note>

## Use Cases

Integrating Figma with MoEngage helps you with the following use cases:

- **Design-led deployment**: Create and iterate on email layouts in Figma, and then import them into MoEngage as production-ready HTML for campaign launch and A/B testing.
- **Design consistency**: The integration preserves brand consistency and design fidelity, which reduces manual coding errors and shortens the time it takes to update a template.

# Integration

Integrating Figma with MoEngage is a three-step process:

1. [Connect Figma in the App Marketplace](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-1-connect-figma-in-the-app-marketplace)
2. [Import a Figma Design into a Campaign](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-2-import-a-figma-design-into-a-campaign)
3. [Create an Email Campaign on MoEngage](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-3-create-an-email-campaign-on-moengage)

## Step 1: Connect Figma in the App Marketplace

To connect Figma in the App Marketplace, perform the following steps:

1. On the left navigation menu in the MoEngage UI, click **App Marketplace**.
2. On the **App marketplace** page, search for **Figma**.
3. Click the **Figma** tile.

   ![The App marketplace page with Figma entered in the search box and the Figma tile in the search results](/images/figma-screenshot-placeholder.png)
4. On the Figma page, click the **Integrate** tab.
5. Under **Channel**, click **\+ Add integration**.

   ![The Integrate tab showing the Channel section and the Add integration option](/images/figma-screenshot-placeholder.png)
6. Under **Connection details**, in the **Connection name** box, type a unique name for the connection.
7. Click **Test**.

   ![The Connection details form with the Connection name box, the Test button, and the Cancel option](/images/figma-screenshot-placeholder.png)

MoEngage connects to Figma through OAuth, so you sign in to Figma and grant access rather than enter a password. Changes to a connection take up to 15 minutes to take effect.

Your connection appears on the **Integrate** tab with its status, connection name, and integration type. After the connection is active, the **Import from Figma** option becomes available in the email content editor.

## Step 2: Import a Figma Design into a Campaign

You import a Figma design while you build the content of an email campaign. To convert a design into an email template, perform the following steps:

1. In **Step 2: Content** of your email campaign, click the **Custom HTML editor** tab.
2. Under **Edit or upload html templates**, click **Import from Figma**.

   ![The Custom HTML editor tab showing the Edit/Paste HTML template, Upload HTML template, and Import from Figma options](/images/figma-screenshot-placeholder.png)
3. Paste the link to your Figma design.
4. Click **Convert**.

   ![The Import from Figma panel with the box for the Figma link and the Convert button](/images/figma-screenshot-placeholder.png)

MoEngage retrieves the design through your Figma authorization and displays the converted HTML in the editor. Review the HTML and edit it before you continue.

If you have not connected Figma, the editor displays the message "Requires the MoEngage Figma plugin. Install it from the App Marketplace." Complete [Step 1](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-1-connect-figma-in-the-app-marketplace) before you import a design.

## Step 3: Create an Email Campaign on MoEngage

To send the converted design to your users, perform the following steps:

1. On the left navigation menu in the MoEngage UI, click **Engage**, and then click **Campaigns**.
2. On the **All campaigns** page, click **\+ Create campaign**.
3. Select **Email**, and then select the delivery type.
4. In **Step 1: Target users**, select the audience for the campaign.
5. In **Step 2: Content**, import your Figma design as described in [Step 2](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-2-import-a-figma-design-into-a-campaign), and then enter the sender details and the subject line.
6. Click **Test Campaign** to send a test email, and confirm that the converted design renders correctly.
7. In **Step 3: Schedule and goals**, set the delivery schedule and the conversion goals.
8. Publish the campaign.

For details on each step, refer to [Create an Email Campaign](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/email/create/create-an-email-campaign).
