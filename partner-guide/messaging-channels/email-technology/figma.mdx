# Introduction

[Figma](https://www.figma.com/) is a collaborative design tool used by design teams to build email layouts. The MoEngage Figma plugin converts a Figma design into a production-ready HTML email template without leaving MoEngage.

You install the plugin as an app in your MoEngage workspace and configure your Figma credentials there. The conversion runs inside MoEngage: when you paste a Figma link into an email campaign and convert it, MoEngage uses your saved credentials to fetch the design from Figma and returns the corresponding HTML.

<Note>
  You do not install anything in Figma for this integration. The plugin is installed in your MoEngage workspace, and the design is imported into MoEngage rather than pushed out from Figma.
</Note>

## Use Cases

Integrating Figma with MoEngage helps you with the following use cases:

- **Rapid, design-led deployment**: Designers create hyper-personalized layouts in Figma, and you import them into MoEngage as production-ready HTML for immediate campaign launch and A/B testing.
- **Guaranteed consistency and efficiency**: The integration preserves brand consistency and design fidelity, eliminating manual coding errors, speeding up template updates, and freeing up development resources.

# Integration

Integrating Figma with MoEngage is a three-step process:

1. [Install the Figma App](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-1-install-the-figma-app)
2. [Import a Figma Design into a Campaign](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-2-import-a-figma-design-into-a-campaign)
3. [Create an Email Campaign on MoEngage](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-3-create-an-email-campaign-on-moengage)

## Step 1: Install the Figma App

To install the Figma app in your MoEngage workspace, perform the following steps:

1. On the left navigation menu in the MoEngage dashboard, click **App Marketplace**.
2. On the App Marketplace page, search for **Figma**.
3. Click the **Figma** tile.
4. On the Figma page, go to the **Integrate** tab and click **\+ Add connection**.
5. Enter the following details:

   | Field | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Connection name** | Name of the Figma connection. |
   | *(pending)* | *(pending)* |

6. Click **Connect**.

Your Figma connection is now integrated, and the **Import from Figma** option becomes available in the email content editor.

## Step 2: Import a Figma Design into a Campaign

You import a Figma design while building the content of an email campaign. To convert a design into an email template, perform the following steps:

1. In **Step 2: Content** of your email campaign, click the **Custom HTML editor** tab.
2. Under **Edit or upload html templates**, click **Import from Figma**.
3. Paste the link to your Figma design.
4. Click **Convert**.

   ![The Custom HTML editor tab showing the Edit/Paste HTML template, Upload HTML template, and Import from Figma options](/images/figma-import-from-figma.png)

MoEngage fetches the design using your saved Figma credentials and displays the converted HTML in the editor. Review the HTML and make any edits you need before you continue.

If you have not installed the Figma app yet, the editor shows the message "Requires the MoEngage Figma plugin. Install it from the App Marketplace." Complete [Step 1](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-1-install-the-figma-app) before you import a design.

## Step 3: Create an Email Campaign on MoEngage

To send the converted design to your users, perform the following steps:

1. On the left navigation menu in the MoEngage dashboard, click **Campaigns**.
2. Click **\+ Create campaign** and select **Email**, and then select the delivery type.
3. In **Step 1: Target users**, select the audience you want to send the campaign to.
4. In **Step 2: Content**, import your Figma design as described in [Step 2](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/email-technology/figma#step-2-import-a-figma-design-into-a-campaign), and then enter the sender details and subject line.
5. Use **Test Campaign** to send yourself a test email and confirm the converted design renders as expected.
6. In **Step 3: Schedule and goals**, set the delivery schedule and conversion goals.
7. Publish the campaign.

For details on each of these steps, refer to [Create an Email Campaign](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/email/create/create-an-email-campaign).
