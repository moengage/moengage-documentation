There is a wide variety of publicly available APIs that can be used with [MoEngage Content APIs](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/getting-started/campaign-content/content-apis). Public APIs allow you to insert custom, real-time data into your messages at send time. This page compiles a list of public APIs across common categories that you can use for content personalisation.

There are many more platforms out there, and this is intended as a starting point rather than an exhaustive list.

Know a platform worth adding? Write to us at [partnerships@moengage.com](mailto:partnerships@moengage.com) and we will review it for inclusion.

<Info>
**Note**

Since these are public APIs, each platform enforces its own rate limits, access models, and usage restrictions. Be sure to review their documentation and consult the API provider about your specific use case before connecting them to MoEngage.
</Info>

Personalise campaigns with upcoming concerts, sports fixtures, theatre shows, and ticket availability near your user's location.

Particularly useful for music-focused personalisation tied to a user's favourite artists. |
| Ticketek | The dominant ticketing platform across Australia and New Zealand, handling major sports leagues, arena concerts, and theatre productions. Contact Ticketek directly to discuss partner access. A third party feature overview is available at [SportsFirst](https://www.sportsfirst.net/sportsapi/ticketek-ticket-api). |
