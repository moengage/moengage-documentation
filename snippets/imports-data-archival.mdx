<Warning>
  **Imported data is subject to archival**

  MoEngage archives inactive users, events, and devices every 14 days and deletes the archived data permanently. Registered users are archived after 365 days without activity or campaign engagement, and anonymous users after 60 days.

  Importing or updating user attributes does not count as activity, so it does not postpone archival. Only events and campaign engagement do.

  Before you import, check the criteria in [Data Archival (Retention) Policy](/user-guide/data/key-concepts/data-archival-retention-policy) to confirm which of your records qualify.
</Warning>
