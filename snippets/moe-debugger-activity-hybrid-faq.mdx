The MoEngage {props.sdk} SDK bundles the native MoEngage Android SDK, so your Android build includes `MoEDebuggerActivity`, a component that supports on-device SDK debugging. Refer to [What is MoEDebuggerActivity?](/developer-guide/android-sdk/troubleshooting-and-faqs/troubleshooting-and-faqs) to understand what it does.

To remove it from your {props.sdk} app, add the following to your Android project's `AndroidManifest.xml`:

```xml
       <activity
                android:name="com.moengage.sdk.debugger.MoEDebuggerActivity"
                tools:node="remove" />

```
