BFSI apps generate high-signal event data every minute — deposits, EMI payments, SIP contributions, policy renewals, claims — but most of it never reaches the user profile. Computed traits turn those raw events into live user attributes you can segment on, personalize with, and branch Flows on, without engineering support or CSV uploads.

This page is a menu. Each row tells you what to build, which computation method to pick, what event data it needs, the business outcome, and how to activate it.

## How Computed Traits Work

Every trait on this page follows the same four-step model. Once you recognize it, the tables below read at a glance.

<CardGroup cols={4}>
  <Card title="1. Start with an event" icon="database">
    A user behavior you already track, such as Deposit Successful or Loan Product Viewed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Choose a method" icon="calculator">
    Count, Aggregation, First/Last Value, or SQL.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Get a trait" icon="user-check">
    A live attribute like Lifetime Deposit Value that updates automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="4. Activate it" icon="rocket">
    Use it in segmentation, Jinja personalization, and Flows.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  **Most traits here are no-code.** The <Badge color="green">Count</Badge> <Badge color="green">Aggregation</Badge> and <Badge color="green">First/Last Value</Badge> methods need no SQL. Reach for <Badge color="purple">SQL</Badge> only for composite scores that combine multiple events, or for ratios such as redemption or completion rate. You can build every no-code trait on this page yourself.
</Tip>

## Browse Traits by Goal

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Customer Value" icon="gem">
    Quantify the depth of each customer's relationship with your bank so you can prioritize service tiers, wealth-management outreach, and premium-card upgrades.

    | Computed Trait | Method | Data Source (Events / Attributes) | Business Use Case | How to Activate |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Lifetime Deposit Value | Aggregation (Sum) | Event: Deposit Successful; Attribute: `amount` | Identify high-net-worth customers for priority banking and dedicated relationship managers. | Personalize private-banking email invites; segment for RM assignment; use in Jinja to greet by tier. |
    | Total Amount Invested | Aggregation (Sum) | Event: Investment Successful; Attribute: `amount` | Bucket users into Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum wealth tiers automatically. | Trigger tier-upgrade campaigns; personalize research reports; feed into RFM. |
    | Total Investments Made | Count | Event: Investment Successful | Activity indicator — active investors vs. one-time depositors. | Reward frequent investors; nurture single-transaction users toward a second investment. |
    | Average Monthly Balance (AMB) | Aggregation (Average) | Event: End-of-Day Balance; Attribute: `balance` | Detect customers falling below the minimum AMB before they are penalized. | Event-triggered nudge before month-end when trending low; educational push for new joiners. |
    | Wallet Share Score | SQL Computation | Investment, Deposit, and Loan events combined with the `declared_income` attribute | Composite score measuring how much of a customer's financial life sits with you vs. competitors. | Prioritize cross-sell for low-share, high-income customers; suppress users already at ceiling. |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Product Affinity" icon="arrows-repeat">
    Move from generic product blasts to precise cross-sell by capturing which products a customer explores, applies for, or shows recurring interest in.

    | Computed Trait | Method | Data Source (Events / Attributes) | Business Use Case | How to Activate |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Last Loan Product Viewed | Last Value | Event: Loan Product Viewed; Attribute: `loan_type` | Re-engage drop-off users with the exact product they last considered (Home, Auto, or Personal). | Dynamic content in email or push using `{{last_loan_product_viewed}}`; retargeting Flow. |
    | Preferred Investment Category | Last Value | Event: Investment Successful; Attribute: `category` (Equity, Debt, Gold, ELSS) | Recommend NFOs and thematic funds aligned to a demonstrated preference. | Content blocks in the newsletter switch by category; suppress irrelevant fund launches. |
    | Distinct Product Categories Held | Aggregation (Count Distinct) | Event: Product Owned Snapshot; Attribute: `product_type` | Identify single-product customers who are ready for cross-sell. | Cross-sell adjacent products to single-product holders; suppress if already at 3 or more products. |
    | Total Card Spend (Last 90 Days) | Aggregation (Sum) | Event: Card Transaction; Attribute: `amount` | Identify heavy card users for premium-card upgrades and reward programs. | Trigger a limit-enhancement offer for high spenders; activation nudge for low spenders. |
    | Loan Pre-Qualified | SQL Computation | Events: Loan Offer Viewed, Documents Submitted; Attributes: `credit_score`, `income` | Composite trait for users who meet pre-approval criteria without applying. | One-tap pre-approved offer in in-app; personalized push ("You're pre-approved for a loan"). |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Risk & Lifecycle" icon="shield-check">
    Use behavioral signals to time compliance nudges, renewal reminders, and retention plays before they become escalations.

    | Computed Trait | Method | Data Source (Events / Attributes) | Business Use Case | How to Activate |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Last Transaction Date | Last Value | Event: Any Transaction; Attribute: `timestamp` | Detect dormancy — use date operators in segmentation to catch users lapsing at 60, 90, or 120 days. | Re-activation campaign at day 60, 90, and 120 with escalating incentives. |
    | Total Transactions (Last 30 Days) | Count | Event: Any Transaction | Activity intensity — a drop in transactions is an early churn-risk signal. | Trigger a check-in nudge when transactions in the last 30 days fall below half of usual activity. |
    | Total Support Tickets Raised | Count | Event: Support Ticket Raised | Identify customers who may be at risk after raising complaints. | NPS follow-up and goodwill offer within 7 days of a resolved ticket. |
    | Days to Policy Expiry | Last Value | Attribute: `policy_expiry_date` on the Policy Purchased event | Trigger renewal campaigns at the right moment for each customer. | Use date operators in segmentation to trigger at 30, 15, and 7 days before expiry. |
    | Policy Renewal Score | SQL Computation | Combines historical renewal count, claims filed, and engagement recency | Composite score predicting renewal likelihood — reserve premium outreach for low scores. | SMS and WhatsApp sequence 30, 15, and 7 days out for low-score renewals only. |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Engagement" icon="signal">
    Encourage digital-channel adoption to lower cost-to-serve and improve NPS, but only for the customers who need the nudge.

    | Computed Trait | Method | Data Source (Events / Attributes) | Business Use Case | How to Activate |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Total Digital Sessions (30 Days) | Count | Event: App Session Started | Identify digital-active vs. dormant users for separate marketing tracks. | Reactivation for zero-session users; feature-adoption nudges for casual users. |
    | Total SIPs Completed | Count | Event: SIP Successful | Celebrate disciplined investors; identify serial missers. | Milestone congratulations at 6, 12, and 24 SIPs; recovery nudge after a miss. |
    | Rewards Points Balance | Aggregation (Sum) | Event: Points Balance Updated; Attribute: `current_balance` | Drive redemption and reduce point-expiry churn. | Jinja personalization — "You have `{{points_balance}}` points expiring in 30 days." |
    | Preferred Login Channel | Last Value | Event: App Session Started; Attribute: `platform` | Optimize the channel mix per user — no push if they only use web banking. | Reachability-aware campaigns; suppress mobile push for web-only users. |
    | Preferred Transaction Channel | Last Value | Event: Transaction Completed; Attribute: `channel` (App, Web, Branch, IVR) | Understand true channel preference and message through the channel a customer actually uses. | Match the campaign channel to the preferred channel; suppress irrelevant channel promotions. |
