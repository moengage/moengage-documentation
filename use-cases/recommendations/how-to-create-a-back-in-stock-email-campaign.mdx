# Introduction

A back-in-stock campaign nudges customers to purchase products that have been restocked. When a customer shows interest in an out-of-stock product, you can add them to a notification list and email them once the product is available again. This helps you manage out-of-stock situations, recover lost revenue, and improve customer engagement.

In this article, we create a back-in-stock campaign that emails customers about products they previously expressed interest in and that have now been restocked, using MoEngage Recommendations.

# Expected Result

Customers who signed up for a restock notification receive an email listing the products they were interested in that are back in stock.

<Note>
  **Prerequisites**

  - Recommendations must be enabled for your MoEngage account. To enable it, contact your Customer Success Manager or email [support@moengage.com](mailto:support@moengage.com).
  - **Notify Me event** — an event generated whenever a customer clicks **Notify Me** on an out-of-stock product.
  - **Stock status** — MoEngage needs the stock status of each product refreshed daily.
</Note>

A back-in-stock campaign requires the following:

1. **A product catalog** — the list of products you want to use in your campaigns. For more information, refer to [Catalogs](/user-guide/content/recommendations/prerequisites/catalogs). For a back-in-stock campaign, the catalog should contain information about each product, such as:
   - *id* — the unique identifier for the product.
   - *title* — the name of the product.
   - *link* — link to the landing page of the product.
   - *image_link* — link to the image of the product.
   - *price* — the price of the product.
   - *stock* — a positive integer when the product is in stock, or zero when it is out of stock.

   The first five fields (*id*, *title*, *link*, *image_link*, and *price*) are mandatory for creating a catalog. The *stock* field is how you determine which products are back in stock.

2. **A way to capture user intent** — you need a mechanism to understand when a user shows interest in a product. Set up an event, such as *Notify Me*, that captures the user's request to be notified when the product is restocked. When a user clicks the **Notify Me** button in your app or website, the *Notify Me* event is triggered in MoEngage with the product details (for example, a `product_id` attribute that identifies the product). For more information, refer to [Map User Actions](/user-guide/content/recommendations/prerequisites/map-user-actions-settings).

3. **A recommendation** — this is where you map the user intent to the product catalog. For more information, refer to [User Actions Model](/user-guide/content/recommendations/basic-recommendations/user-actions-model).

4. **The recommendation used in your campaign** — once the recommendation is created, you can use it in your email campaigns, as described in the next section.

# Create an Email Campaign

## Step 1: Target Users

Create an email campaign and define its trigger criteria. For more information on creating an email campaign, refer to [Create an Email Campaign](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/email/create/create-an-email-campaign).

## Step 2: Content

In the content step, add the recommendation you created to list the back-in-stock products the user was interested in:

1. Click the **Rows** tab to add a recommendation (labeled *product set* in the email editor) to the email content.
2. Choose **Product Sets** from the dropdown.
3. In the screen that appears, choose the product set (recommendation) and the layout of the row, map the fields from the product set to the fields in the layout, and click **Create**.

   <img
     src="/images/back-in-stock-add-product-set.gif"
     alt="Choosing a product set, selecting a row layout, and mapping product fields in the email editor"
   />

4. Click **Preview** to check that the product set loads as expected.
5. Click **Next**.

For more information, refer to [Using Recommendations in Email](/user-guide/content/recommendations/personalize-campaign-with-recommendations/using-recommendations-in-email).

## Step 3: Schedule and Goals

1. Add the delivery schedule and delivery controls for the campaign.
2. Click **Publish**.

# Conclusion

In this article, we created a back-in-stock email campaign that uses a recommendation to surface restocked products to customers who signed up to be notified. To see how your campaign performs, head to [Email Analytics and Info](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/email/analyze/email-analytics-and-info).
