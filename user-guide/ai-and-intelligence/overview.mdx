MoEngage AI features fall into two groups.

**Merlin AI** is the generative layer you interact with directly. Describe what you want in plain language and get campaign copy, banners, in-app templates, audience filters, flows, or personalization code back.

**Intelligence features** are models that run inside your campaigns. They forecast what your users will do, then decide which message, channel, and time to use for each one.

<Note>
  Campaign Insights Agent, Merlin AI Segment Assist, Custom Agents, and Campaign Decisioning are Early Access features. To enable them for your workspace, contact your MoEngage Customer Success Manager (CSM) or [raise a support ticket](/user-guide/contact-support/raise-a-support-ticket-through-moengage-dashboard).
</Note>

## Choose the Right Merlin AI Surface

Several Merlin AI features overlap. Which one to use depends on where you are working and how much you want the AI to handle.

- [Merlin AI Studio](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/merlin-ai-studio) is a standalone hub that opens from the top navigation bar. It hosts four single-purpose agents — Copywriter, Designer, Segmentation Assist, and Jinja Assist — plus a gallery of example prompts. Use it to generate an asset before you start building a campaign.
- **In-context assistants** appear inside the editor you are already using. [Flows Assist](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/merlin-ai-flows-assist) works on the flows canvas, and the [In-App Template Generator](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/merlin-ai-in-app-template-generator/merlin-ai-in-app-template-generator) works while you build an in-app message. Use these when you are mid-campaign and do not want to switch pages.
- [Custom Agents](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/custom-agents/custom-agents-overview) let you build a multi-step agent, assign it tools, and chain agents together so one delegates to others. Use this when a single-purpose generator is not enough.
- [MoEngage MCP Server](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/moengage-mcp-server) brings MoEngage into an external AI client. Use it when you would rather work from your own AI tool than from the MoEngage dashboard.

## Where to Open Each Surface

Most Merlin AI features appear inside the campaign or flow editor for the channel you are working in. These four open from their own place in the dashboard:

| Surface | Where to open it |
| --- | --- |
| [Merlin AI Studio](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/merlin-ai-studio) | Top navigation bar > **Merlin AI Studio** |
| [Proactive Insights](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/proactive-insights) | Sidebar > **Analyze** > **Proactive Insights** |
| [Campaign Insights Agent](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/campaign-insights-agent) | The campaign analytics page, when the campaign has stats for the selected time range |
| [Merlin AI Flows Assist](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/merlin-ai-flows-assist) | The flows canvas editor |
