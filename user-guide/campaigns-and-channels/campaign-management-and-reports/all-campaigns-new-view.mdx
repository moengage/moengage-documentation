## Overview

The **All Campaigns** page is the central hub for viewing, organizing, and managing every campaign in your MoEngage account. From this page you can search for specific campaigns, filter and sort the campaign list, customize which information you see, and perform actions on one or many campaigns at once.

This guide covers every element on the page, including the new **Views**, **Dynamic Columns**, **Filters**, and **Bulk Actions** capabilities, along with step-by-step navigation instructions and common use cases.

<Info>
  The capabilities such as  Views, Dynamic Columns, Advanced Filters, Bulk Actions, and Multi-Team Selection are part of the new All Campaigns page and are being rolled out in phases. If you don't see these capabilities on your dashboard, reach out to MoEngage Support or your Customer Success Manager (CSM) to enable it.
</Info>

## Use Cases

The All Campaigns page supports several recurring business needs for teams that run campaigns at scale:

- **Focus on the campaigns that matter to you**: When you own only a subset of channels or campaigns, a general list adds noise. Saved **Views** let you keep a personal, always-ready slice of the campaign list so you spend less time reapplying the same filters every day.
- **Keep a team aligned on the same set of campaigns**: Sharing a **Public** view across one or more teams gives everyone a common, consistent picture of the campaigns they're responsible for, without each member recreating it.
- **Tailor the information to a role**: Different roles care about different data. **Dynamic Columns** let each user surface the metrics relevant to their work and hide the rest, making the table easier to scan and act on.
- **Land on the right place every time**: Setting a **Default view** removes repetitive navigation, so the campaigns you monitor most are the first thing you see when you open the page.
- **Prepare for reviews and stakeholder updates quickly**: Bulk **Tag**, **Download**, and **Report** actions turn a large campaign list into a shareable summary or a detailed performance report in a few clicks, supporting recurring reporting cycles.
- **Manage many campaigns safely at scale**: Bulk **Pause**, **Stop**, and **Resume** apply an operation across many campaigns at once while respecting each campaign's eligibility, reducing manual, one-by-one effort and the risk of acting on the wrong campaign.
- **Declutter your workspace**: Pinning, unpinning, and reordering views keeps daily navigation focused on the tabs each user actually uses.

## Views

### What Is a View?

A **View** is a saved combination of **filters + columns + search term + visibility setting** that lets you define your own way of looking at the campaign list. Instead of re-applying the same filters every time you visit the page, you save them once as a View and select it whenever you want to see that specific slice of campaigns.

For example, a marketer who only cares about their own active Push and Email campaigns can create a View that always shows exactly that, without touching a filter again.

### System (Default) Views

When you load the All Campaigns page for the first time, MoEngage provides a set of built-in **system views**:

- **All**: every campaign, regardless of status.
- **Active**: campaigns currently running.
- **Schedule**: campaigns that are scheduled to send in the future.
- **Drafts**: campaigns saved but not yet activated.
- **Under Review/Rejected**: campaigns pending approval or rejected during review, if [Campaign Approval Workflow](/user-guide/settings/account/team-management/campaign-approval-workflow#enable-campaign-approval-workflow-for-campaigns) is enabled for your workspace.

These system views cannot be deleted, and the **All** view specifically cannot be unpinned/removed from your tab bar. For more information refer to [Manage Views](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/campaign-management-and-reports/all-campaigns-new-view#manage-views).

### View Ownership: Private vs. Public

Views are created at the **individual user level**, every user can build their own set of views, and each view can be marked as one of two visibility types:

- **Private**: only visible to and editable by the user who created it. No one else in the workspace can see it.
- **Public (Team-based)**: shared with one or more teams. Every member of the selected team(s) will see this view appear on their own All Campaigns page.

<Note>
  If your workspace is **not** team-based, marking a view as Public shares it with everyone in the workspace.
</Note>

A single user can create and own **multiple** views. Refer to [View Limits](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/campaign-management-and-reports/all-campaigns-new-view#view-limits) for the workspace-wide cap.

### Create a View

There are two ways to create a view:

**Method 1: Create from an existing filter**

1. Go to any tab (for example, the **All** tab).
2. Apply your desired filters, search term, and/or column selection.
3. Click **Save as view**. You'll be prompted to configure the view (view the steps below).
      <img alt="all_campaigns_5a.gif"  src="/images/all_campaigns_5a.gif"/>

**Method 2: Create from scratch**

1. Click the views dropdown (the down-arrow icon next to your tabs) and select **+ New view**.
2. This opens the **Create view** panel with a blank/default configuration.
3. Configure your filters, columns, and search term as needed, exactly as you would from an existing tab.
    <img alt="all_campaigns_6a.gif"  src="/images/all_campaigns_6a.gif"/>

4. Once configured, click **Create view**.
5. The view is created and automatically loads, showing:

   - The filters that are applied

  <Note>
    Refresh rate

    The system (default) views (All, Active, Schedule, Drafts, Under Review/Rejected) refresh their campaign counts every **1 minute**. Custom views that you create refresh every **60 minutes**. This is because a custom view can include many filters, and running those queries against the database in real time, at scale, could slow down performance for everyone. If the campaign count in your custom view looks out of date, it should catch up within 60 minutes.
  </Note>

#### Manage Views

Click **Manage views** (found in the views dropdown) to open a panel where you can control every view available to you — private, team-shared, and system views.

   <img alt="manage_views.gif"  src="/images/manage_views.gif" />

<Note>
  All view management actions described below (creating, editing, pinning, reordering, and setting a default) apply at the individual user level, not at the workspace level. Each user manages their own set of views independently, so changes you make here won't affect what other users see or how their views are configured.
</Note>

From **Manage views**, you can:

- **Search for a view** using the search box at the top.
- **Reorder views**: drag views using the handle icon to change the order in which they appear as tabs. This ordering is personal to you.
- **Pin/unpin views**: views under **Selected views** are the ones currently pinned as tabs on your main page. Views under **Other views** exist but are not pinned. Click the pin icon to move a view in or out of your pinned tab bar.
  - The **All** view is always pinned and cannot be unpinned.
  - All other views (including system views like Under Review/Rejected) can be freely pinned or unpinned based on your preference.
- **Set a default view**: use the three-dot (⋮) menu next to a view to mark it as **Default**. The default view is the one that automatically loads every time you navigate to the All Campaigns page (for example, after navigating away and returning). The current default is indicated with a **Default** badge. A view must be **pinned** before it can be set as your default, if a view is unpinned, the option to mark it as default won't be available.
- **Edit a view**: use the three-dot menu to modify a view's filters, columns, or visibility. System views cannot be edited.
- **Delete a view**: also available from the three-dot menu. You can only delete views that you created yourself; system views and views created by other users cannot be deleted.
- Click **Apply changes** to save your pinning/reordering updates, or **Cancel** to discard them.

<Note>
  **Pinned views vs. session views:** If you temporarily bring up a view that isn't pinned (for instance, by applying a one-off filter), it will appear in your tab bar only for that session. Once you navigate away to another page and come back, only your pinned views (and your default view) will be shown again.
</Note>

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Update view** | Saves your changes to the current view, overwriting its previous configuration. |

### Change Visibility (Private ↔ Public)

You can change a view's visibility at any time:

1. Open **Manage views**.
2. Select the view and choose to edit it.
3. Change the **View visibility** setting:
   - **Private → Public**: If your workspace uses teams, you'll need to select which team(s) to share it with. The **Share with teams** only lists the teams associated with that specific view, not every team you belong to.
    If there are no teams, the view becomes visible to the entire workspace.
   - **Public → Private**: The view becomes visible only to you again; other users lose access.
4. When switching to Public in a team-based workspace, all currently selected teams are pre-selected by default. You can deselect any team you no longer want to share the view with.

### View Limits

- A workspace can have a maximum of **100 views in total**, combined across all users.
- Once this limit is reached, no user in the workspace can create a new view until an existing one is deleted. You'll see a message indicating the workspace's view limit has been exhausted.

### Multi-Team Selection

Previously, users belonging to multiple teams could only view campaigns for **one** team at a time or **all** teams, there was no way to see a subset. You can:

- **Select multiple specific teams** (rather than only "one" or "all") to filter the campaign list down to exactly the teams you care about. This is especially useful when building a view for a specific group of teams. For example, selecting Team A and Team B together, then saving that combination as a shared view for those teams.

## Show Archived Campaigns

At the top of the campaign list, a **Show archived campaigns** toggle lets you decide whether archived campaigns appear alongside your other campaigns.

* **Toggle ON**: Archived campaigns are included in the campaign list along with your other campaigns (Sent, Scheduled, Active, Draft, and so on). They aren't shown in a separate group, they appear inline with the rest of the campaigns and follow the same [default sorting](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/campaign-management-and-reports/all-campaigns-new-view#default-sorting) (**Last modified**, newest first). Because archived campaigns often haven't been modified recently, they may appear lower down the list, or higher up if they were archived recently.
* **Toggle OFF**: Archived campaigns are hidden from the list.

There is **no time-based restriction** on which archived campaigns are shown. Every campaign in the **Archive** state is included when the toggle is on, regardless of when it was archived. **Archive** is a campaign status, not a separate storage tier.

<Note>
  The **Show archived campaigns** toggle affects only the visibility of archived campaigns in the current list. It does not archive or unarchive any campaign on its own.
</Note>

### Auto-archival of Campaigns in Final States

Campaigns that reach a **final state** are automatically moved to the **Archive** state **6 months** after the date they entered that state. The final states covered by auto-archival are:

* **Sent**
* **Stopped**
* **Expired**

For example, a one-time campaign that was **sent on 21 July 2026** will be auto-archived on **21 January 2027**. Once auto-archived, the campaign no longer appears in the default view as a Sent campaign — it appears only when the **Show archived campaigns** toggle is turned on.

<Note>
  * Auto-archival applies only to campaigns in the **Sent**, **Stopped**, or **Expired** states. Campaigns in **Active**, **Scheduled**, **Draft**, or **Under Review** states are not affected.
  * If you rely on Sent campaigns being visible in your reports or views, make sure to turn on **Show archived campaigns** when reviewing older campaigns (older than 6 months from their final-state date).
</Note>

## Dynamic Columns

Previously, every user saw the same fixed set of columns on the All Campaigns page. **Dynamic Columns** lets each user personalize exactly which data points they want displayed. This is a **per-user** setting.
   <img alt="dynamic_columns.png"  src="/images/dynamic_columns.png" />
### Manage Columns
    <img alt="manage_columns.png"  src="/images/manage_columns.png" width="50%"  />
1. Click the **column selection icon** near the filter bar to open the **Column selection** panel.
2. The panel is split into two lists:
   - **Selected columns**: currently visible on your table. Each has:
     - A **drag handle** (⠿) to reorder columns, the order here determines the left-to-right order in the table.
     - An **eye icon**: click to hide (remove) that column from the view. Once removed, the column moves to **Available columns**.
   - **Available columns**: columns not currently shown. Each has a **crossed-out eye icon**: click to add (show) that column,which moves it into **Selected columns**.
3. Use the **Search columns** box to quickly find a specific column by name.
4. Click **Apply** to save your column selection to the current view, or **Cancel** to discard changes.

### Available Column Options

Depending on your data, columns you can add include (non-exhaustive): Name, Channel, Status, First Send Date, Performance, Engagement, Last modified, Send, Delivery, Open Rate, Conversions, Approved by, Trigger event name, Custom segment, Scheduled on, Created on, Estimated audience count, and Team (visible only in team-based workspaces).

### Default Columns per Tab
 Each system tab now ships with a **different, tab-relevant** default column set, rather than one identical set across all tabs. This makes each tab more readable and surfaces only the information that matters for that campaign state.
<Note>
  - If you don't want the tailored column sets, you can toggle back to a simpler, unified view using the **Switch to classic view** provided at the top of the table.
  - **Estimated audience count** refreshes every **24 hours** and reflects an estimate, not the exact audience size at send time. If you need an accurate count, check this closer to when the campaign is scheduled to go out.
  -  For a periodic (recurring) campaign, hover over its **First Send Date** to see when the **next instance** is scheduled to go out and when the **last instance** was sent. This hover detail is only available for periodic campaigns.
</Note>

All views, system (default) views and any custom views you create, are sorted by **Last modified** by default.

## Bulk Actions

Bulk Actions let you perform the same operation on multiple campaigns at once, instead of opening each campaign individually.
   <img alt="bulk_actions.png"  src="/images/bulk_actions.png"  />

  <Note>
    You can select up to **50 campaigns** at a time for a bulk action. If you need to act on more than 50 campaigns, run the bulk action in multiple batches (for example, split 100 campaigns into two batches of 50).
  </Note>

### Select Campaigns

1. Use the checkboxes on the left of each row to select one or more campaigns.
2. Once at least one campaign is selected, an action bar appears showing **Selected (X/Total)** along with the available actions.
3. Use **Show selected** (toggle) to filter the table down to only the campaigns you've currently selected. This is useful for reviewing your selection before running an action.
4. Use **Clear selection** to deselect all campaigns and reset the action bar.

### Available Bulk Actions

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| **Tag** | Apply one or more tags to all selected campaigns at once. |
| **Download** | Downloads a CSV file containing the columns currently visible on your screen, for the selected campaigns. This is a quick export of only the on-screen data. |
| **Report** | Navigates you to the Reports page with the selected campaigns and their channels pre-loaded — no need to reselect campaigns. Reports include a much more detailed set of columns than Download, such as content and delivery-funnel details. |
| **Archive** | Archives all eligible selected campaigns. Campaigns not eligible for archiving are skipped. |
| **Pause** | Pauses all eligible selected campaigns (e.g., active campaigns). If some selected campaigns can't be paused (for example, they're already sent), only the eligible ones are paused, and you're shown how many will be affected before confirming (e.g., "3 of 6 campaigns can be paused").<img alt="pause_campaigns.png"  src="/images/pause_campaigns.png" width="50%" /> |
| **Stop** | Stops all eligible selected campaigns, following the same eligibility logic as Pause. |
| **Resume** | Resumes previously paused campaigns, where eligible. |

### Tab-Dependent Availability

The set of bulk actions shown in the action bar changes depending on which tab (view) you're in, since not every action makes sense for every campaign state. For example, **Resume** never appears in the **Schedule** tab because a scheduled campaign hasn't started yet, and **Delete** only appears in the **Drafts** tab because it's the only tab where all campaigns are safely removable.

The table below shows which bulk actions are available in each system tab:

| Bulk Action | All | Active | Schedule | Drafts | Under Review / Rejected |
| ----------- | :-: | :----: | :------: | :----: | :---------------------: |
| Tag         | ✅  |   ✅   |    ✅    |   —    |           ✅            |
| Download    | ✅  |   ✅   |    ✅    |   ✅   |           ✅            |
| Report      | ✅  |   ✅   |    —     |   —    |           —             |
| Archive     | ✅  |   ✅   |    —     |   —    |           ✅            |
| Pause       | ✅  |   ✅   |    —     |   —    |           —             |
| Stop        | ✅  |   ✅   |    ✅    |   —    |           —             |
| Resume      | ✅  |   ✅   |    —     |   —    |           —             |
| Delete      | —   |   —    |    —     |   ✅   |           —             |

<Note>
  - On the **All** tab, every bulk action is available. Eligibility is evaluated per campaign at execution time — for example, if you select a mix of Sent and Active campaigns and click **Pause**, only the Active ones are paused, and you're shown a confirmation like "3 of 6 campaigns can be paused" before running.
  - On tabs that contain only one class of campaigns (Schedule, Drafts, Under Review/Rejected), the action bar is narrowed to just the actions that make sense for that state. For instance, **Report** doesn't appear on the **Schedule** tab because scheduled campaigns haven't sent yet and have no reporting data.
  - **Delete** is only exposed as a bulk action on the **Drafts** tab, in line with the row-level menu.
</Note>

**Difference between Download and Report:**
- **Download** gives you the basic set of columns you already see on the UI, in CSV format.
- **Report** gives you a full, detailed report including content and delivery-funnel information for the selected campaigns and channels.

## Permissions and Roles

The actions available to you on the All Campaigns page — both in the ⋮ menu on individual campaigns and in the Bulk Actions bar — depend on your **role** in the workspace. If your role doesn't have permission for an action, the action will not appear.

There are two categories of roles:

- **System roles**: pre-defined roles that ship with MoEngage (Admin, Manager, Marketer, Developer, Analyst). Their permissions are fixed and cannot be modified.
- **Custom roles**: roles configured by your workspace's Admin with a specific set of permissions. What a user with a custom role can do depends entirely on which permissions were granted when that role was set up.
For more information on roles and how to configure them, refer to [Users and Roles](/user-guide/settings/account/team-management/users-and-roles).

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Views, Dynamic Columns, or Multi-Team Selection on my All Campaigns page?">
    These capabilities are being rolled out in phases and may not be available on your account yet. Reach out to your Customer Success Manager (CSM) to request access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the campaign count on my custom view look outdated?">
    Out-of-the-box system views refresh their counts every 1 minute, while custom views refresh every 60 minutes. This is intentional: custom views can include many filters, and running those queries in real time at scale would slow down the page for everyone. Give it up to 60 minutes to catch up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can other users see or be affected by changes I make to my views?">
    No. Views and view management (creating, editing, pinning, reordering, setting a default) are scoped to your individual user account, unless you explicitly share a view as Public with a team. Your default view and pinned tabs are personal to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I set a view as my default?">
    A view must be pinned before it can be set as your default. Pin the view first from Manage Views, then use the three-dot menu to mark it as Default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see all my teams in the Share with teams list when making a view public?">
    The Share with teams list only shows the teams that were selected when the view was built. If you have access to more teams than were used to define the view, only the teams associated with that view will appear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many campaigns can I select for a bulk action?">
    You can select up to 50 campaigns per bulk action. For larger batches, split your selection and run the action multiple times.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the estimated audience count on the Schedule tab exact?">
    No. It's an estimate that refreshes every 24 hours, not the exact audience size at send time. Check closer to send time if you need precise numbers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
