---
title: "Why Do the Failed Counts in the Campaign Stats Donut and the Error Breakdown Not Match?"
description: "MoEngage reconciles the campaign stats donut and the error breakdown so failure counts always match."
---

## Problem

The failed numbers in the campaign stats donut and the error breakdown do not match.

## Explanation

The mismatch happens when a user is blocked by a Campaign Control, such as Frequency Capping or Minimum Delay, or by a sending or content error, but that user was never counted in Attempted to begin with. When Attempted excludes these users but the error breakdown still lists them under a failure reason, the failed count in the campaign stats donut (calculated from Attempted) does not match the total in the error breakdown.

MoEngage counts every qualified user as Attempted from the start, and adds any user later blocked to Failed To Send instead of leaving them out. This keeps Attempted, Sent, and Failed To Send consistent, so the failed count in the campaign stats donut matches the error breakdown.

Refer to [Delivery Metrics](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/mobile-push/analyze/push-analytics-and-info#campaign-performance-stats) for more information on how Attempted and Failed To Send are calculated.
