---
title: "Why Do the Failed Counts in the Campaign Stats Donut and the Error Breakdown Not Match?"
description: "For push campaigns, MoEngage reconciles the campaign stats donut and the error breakdown so failure counts always match."
---

## Problem

The failed numbers in the campaign stats donut and the error breakdown do not match.

## Explanation

For push campaigns, MoEngage counts every qualified user as Attempted from the start, and adds any user that a Campaign Control (such as Frequency Capping or Minimum Delay), a sending error, or a content error later blocks to Failed To Send. As a result, Attempted always equals Sent plus Failed To Send, and the failed count in the campaign stats donut matches the error breakdown.

Campaigns created before MoEngage enabled this calculation for your account continue to calculate failures as Sent minus Delivered. This formula excludes certain blocked users, such as those blocked by Frequency Capping or Minimum Delay, from both Attempted and Failed To Send, which is why the donut and the error breakdown can show different failure counts on these campaigns.

Refer to [Funnel Logic Versions](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/mobile-push/analyze/push-analytics-and-info#funnel-logic-versions) for the full comparison.
