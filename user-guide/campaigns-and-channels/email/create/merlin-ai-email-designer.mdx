# Introduction

Merlin AI Email Designer helps you go from a campaign idea to a ready-to-edit HTML email without leaving campaign creation. Instead of briefing a designer or hand-coding personalisation tags, you describe what you want, or point to a screenshot of an existing email and Merlin AI Email Designer generates HTML that is:

* Structured from your brief, your reference screenshot, or the audience segment already selected for the campaign
* Personalised using MoEngage Jinja syntax rather than generic template syntax
* Checked before it's shown to you, covering rendering, personalisation fallbacks, and compliance

<Info>
  Generation typically takes about 50 seconds. This is expected, Merlin AI Email Designer runs a generate-and-review pass before showing you the result, rather than returning a single unchecked draft.
</Info>

Merlin AI Email Designer is available from four places once you're in [Step 2: Content](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/email/create/create-an-email-campaign#step-2-content) of email campaign creation or template management:

* **Content step, first visit**: The Step 2:Content defaults to manual options such as **Drag and drop editor**, **Custom HTML editor**, **My saved templates**, **Imported templates**. Above these, a banner reads *"High-converting brand emails, engineered by Merlin AI"*. Click **Create using Email Designer** on that banner to start the AI flow instead. Once you've generated content, this same spot instead shows *"Looking for more changes? Your chat history is saved for this session."* — click **Edit using Email Designer** to reopen your chat and switch between saved versions.
* **Template shortcut**: Select a template manually using **Apply to campaign**, then click **Edit via Email Designer**. The chat opens with a **Load my manual edits** prompt already suggested, select it to bring your manual changes into the AI session before continuing.
* **Template Manager, editing an existing template**: Hover over a template and select **Edit using Email Designer**. The Merlin AI Email Designer panel slides out with the template attached as a chip above the chat input (showing the template name and **Template** label). Unlike the Template shortcut above, no prompt is submitted automatically, type your own brief to start refining the attached template.

   {/* SCREENSHOT: Template Manager empty state with template attached as a chip above the chat input, and the "Create high converting content using Email Designer" placeholder panel on the right */}
* **Template Manager, creating a new template** — go to **Content > Templates** and click **+ Create template**. This opens a two-step **Create template** flow (**1 Choose template**, **2 Customise**). At the Choose template step, the same Merlin AI banner appears above the manual template options — click **Create using Email Designer** to build the new template with AI instead of starting from a blank or pre-built layout.

<Info>
  Once you apply a generated email to your campaign, it lands in the standard content step editor, where **+ Variation** and **+ Locale** controls next to the version tabs let you add further A/B variations or locales directly, without reopening the chat. A **User distribution** link controls the traffic split across variations. The same screen also shows a raw HTML **Template Editor**, so you can likely keep editing the applied content manually from there too — this hasn't been explicitly confirmed for AI-generated content specifically, so treat it as inferred until verified.
</Info>

## Generate an Email from a Written Brief

Perform the following steps to generate an email from a written brief:

1. Navigate to [**Step 2: Content**](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/email/create/create-an-email-campaign#step-2-content) of email campaign creation journey.
2. At the Content step, click **Create using Email Designer** on the Merlin AI banner (above the manual editor tabs).

   {/* SCREENSHOT: Content step showing the manual editor tabs and the "Create using Email Designer" Merlin AI banner above them */}
3. In the chat input, type your brief, or select one of the starter options: **Festival discount**, **Newsletter**, **Event invitation**, or **Welcome email**. Include the campaign type, offer, and tone wherever possible. For example: *"Create a 48-hour flash sale email for our Summer Footwear collection, 30% off, urgent tone."*
4. Type **@** in the chat input to insert keywords such as content blocks or personalisation attributes directly into your brief.
5. If Merlin AI Email Designer needs more information before it can generate the email, for example, a missing offer or call-to-action, it asks for the missing detail first.
6. Merlin AI Email Designer generates the HTML email along with **Subject** and **Preview text**, shown in the Preview pane. Both fields are required, editable, and show a live character count.

   {/* SCREENSHOT: Preview pane with generated email, subject/preview text fields with character counters */}

   You can always edit the **Subject** or **Preview text** directly by typing into the field, instead of asking Merlin AI Email Designer to change it for you. Doing so replaces the usual **Cancel / Save template / Apply to campaign** row at the bottom with an **Unsaved changes** indicator and **Discard** / **Save** buttons — use **Save** to keep your manual edit before continuing.
7. Each prompt or refinement you submit is saved as a new, named version. Merlin AI Email Designer titles it after what the prompt changed (for example, *Exclusive Event Invitation Campaign (Version 1)* becoming *Personalized Event Name Campaign (Version 4)* after further refinements), rather than a plain sequence number. Every version stays listed in the version dropdown and as cards in the chat history, so you can reopen or restore an earlier version at any point in the session. A version already applied to your campaign is labeled **Used in campaign** on its card.

<Warning>
  The refresh icon next to the version dropdown is **Start a new chat**. Clicking it opens a **Restart chat** confirmation — confirming clears your current drafts and chat history, so save any version you want to keep (see [Save your email as a template](#save-your-email-as-a-template)) before restarting.
</Warning>

<Info>
  - For more on setting up delivery types, targeting, and scheduling outside of content generation, see [Create an Email Campaign](https://www.moengage.com/docs/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/email/create/create-an-email-campaign).
  - Output quality can vary across sessions for the same prompt. Review the generated email carefully before publishing, even for a well-specified brief. Your chat history is saved for the session, so you can return and refine or switch versions later.
</Info>

## Generate an Email from a Reference Screenshot

Perform the following steps to generate an email from a screenshot of an existing email:

1. In the chat input, click **+** to open the attachment menu, then select **Upload image** and choose a screenshot. Use this when you have a reference design (an old email, a competitor's, or a mockup) you want Merlin to match rather than design from scratch.
2. Merlin AI Email Designer analyzes the attachment and generates HTML matching its layout, colors, typography, and structure.

<Info>
  Image-based generation currently works best for hero images. When a screenshot is used as a layout reference, Merlin AI Email Designer picks up brand colors reliably, but exact layout, alignment, and placement match the reference only partially — review the result closely.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Generating an email directly from a Figma design is not currently supported. Paste a screenshot of the design instead.
</Warning>

## Attach a Template, Content Block, or URL

Besides screenshots, the same **+** menu in the chat input lets you attach a template, a content block, or a URL as reference material for your brief:

1. Click **+** to open the attachment menu — **Upload image**, **Attach template**, and **Attach content block**.
2. To attach a template, select **Attach template**. In the dialog, search by name or browse **Folders and templates**, filter by **All**, **Saved templates**, or **API templates**, and sort by **Last modified**. Select a template and click **Attach**. Use this to build within an approved, on-brand layout instead of having Merlin design one from scratch — for example, attach a brand template and ask it to adapt the copy for a new offer.
3. To attach a content block, select **Attach content block**. Search or browse the **Content blocks** list on the left (grouped by type, such as **HTML**) — selecting a block shows its preview side by side on the right, including the **Block label** (in the form `{{ ContentBlock['<block_name>'] }}`) and a table of its available fields and their Jinja paths, such as `{{ result.email }}`, `{{ result.tier }}`, or `{{ result.discount_pct }}` for a block backed by dynamic data. Use this to reuse an approved footer, legal text, or a data-driven section instead of letting Merlin write its own version.

   Toggle **Insert only the content of the content block** if you want to paste in a static copy of the block's content. Enabling this disconnects the content from the source content block — any future updates made to that content block will no longer be reflected in this campaign. Leave it off to keep the live reference instead.
4. To attach a URL, paste the link directly into the chat input — it's picked up automatically and shown as a **URL** chip, without needing the **+** menu.
5. Each attachment appears as its own chip above the chat input (showing a thumbnail or icon, its name, and its type — **Template**, **Content block**, or **URL**). You can attach more than one at a time and reference all of them in a single prompt — for example: *"Use this and create a campaign for Apple iPhones using a 20% off festival coupon."*

## Add Personalisation with User Attributes

Perform the following steps to personalize a generated email with user attributes, such as first name, loyalty tier, or last purchase date:

1. Ask Merlin AI Email Designer to personalize a section of the email, or type **@** to insert a known attribute directly. For example: *"Personalize the greeting with the customer's first name."*
2. If the attribute key isn't recognized, provide the exact key name used in your workspace when prompted.
3. Merlin AI Email Designer inserts the token using MoEngage Jinja syntax with a fallback value — for example, an event name attribute renders as `{{ EventAttribute['Name'] }}`.

<Info>
  Always verify personalisation tokens before publishing, particularly for ambiguous cases.
</Info>

## Generate A/B Test Variations

Perform the following steps to generate multiple variations of a generated email for A/B testing:

1. Ask Merlin AI Email Designer to create variations — for example, *"Create 3 A/B test variants"* — or select this as a follow-up suggestion after a generation.
2. Merlin AI Email Designer generates each variation around a distinct strategy — for example, a control focused on the core offer, a version testing urgency with a countdown-style subject line, and a version leading with an emotive, lifestyle-driven benefit — and explains the strategy behind each in the chat.
3. Compare the variations using the **Variation 1** / **Variation 2** / **Variation 3** tabs at the top of the Preview pane. Each tab has its own Subject, Preview text, and Content.
4. Use follow-up suggestions to iterate across variations — for example, **Which variant is likely to perform best?**, **Combine Variation 2 and 3**, **Apply Variation 2's subject line to all**, or **Make Variation 1 more professional**.

## Generate Locale Variants

Perform the following steps to generate locale-adapted versions of an email:

1. Ask Merlin AI Email Designer to localize the email for a specific audience — for example, *"I also need these localized for our German market. Let's add a German locale for these."*
2. Select the target locale from the **Locale** dropdown that appears. Locales correspond to a user property already configured in your workspace (for example, a locale named **German** maps to users whose **Locale Language** property is set to **German**). Search the list, check one or more locales, or click **+ Add Locale** to add one not yet listed. Submitting your selection shows as a compact **Add Locale `<name>`** chip in the chat.

   {/* SCREENSHOT: Locale dropdown with search, checkboxes for existing locales, and "+ Add Locale" option */}
3. If the locale name you typed doesn't match a configured locale, Merlin AI Email Designer tells you it's unable to find it and asks you to select the correct one from the dropdown instead.
4. Choose whether to **translate the existing content** into that locale or **generate new content** specifically for that audience — or give Merlin AI Email Designer a specific prompt to guide the adaptation, such as tone changes.
5. Merlin AI Email Designer generates the locale-adapted content and explains its language and tone choices in the chat.
6. In the Preview pane, locale tabs (for example, **Default** / **German**) appear above the variation tabs, so you can review each locale-and-variation combination independently.

<Info>
  Always verify translated and locale-adapted content before publishing, particularly for ambiguous or unconfigured locale selections.
</Info>

## Work with a Generated Message

Every message Merlin AI Email Designer sends includes a row of actions you can use directly on that message:

* **Edit** — adjust the message.
* **Copy** — copy the message content.
* **Regenerate** — ask for a new attempt.
* **Thumbs up / thumbs down** — rate the response. This feedback helps improve future generations.

Below each generated message, expand **Reasoning** to see how Merlin AI Email Designer arrived at that version, and check **Follow-ups** for one-click next steps. Follow-up suggestions are contextual — for example, **Make it more concise**, **Punchier subject line**, or **Stronger call-to-action**.

Each generation is explained in plain language rather than labeled report sections — typically a summary line followed by a bolded **Strategy highlights** list (for example, **Visual**, **Tone**, or **Language**, **Tone Shift**) describing the specific choices made, and a prompt to review the Preview pane or provide a follow-up.

<Note>
  Merlin AI Email Designer can make mistakes. Verify the generated email before taking any action on it, such as publishing.
</Note>

## Refine a Generated Email

Perform the following steps to make changes to an email you've already generated:

1. In the chat input, describe the change you want. For example: *"Change the CTA color to blue"* or *"Add a second product row."*
2. Merlin AI Email Designer applies only the requested change and leaves the rest of the email untouched.
3. If your request is open to interpretation — for example, *"make it feel more premium"* — Merlin AI Email Designer asks a clarifying question before making any changes.
4. Open the version dropdown at any point to jump straight to an earlier named version — for example, back to *Exclusive Event Invitation Campaign (Version 1)* before your later refinements — without losing the newer versions.

<Info>
  Merlin AI Email Designer does not currently summarize or compare what changed between two versions. Review each version's preview directly to see the difference.
</Info>

## Save Your Email as a Template

Save the current version as a template using **Save as New Template** (top right of the panel) or **Save template** (bottom of the panel, next to Cancel and Apply to campaign) — the label shown depends on where you are in the flow. Either opens a dialog where you:

1. Enter a **Template Name** (up to 100 characters).
2. Enter a **Sender Name**.
3. Click **Save as new template**. This button stays disabled until both fields are filled in.

The email content, subject line, and preview text of the current version are saved together as a reusable template you can use for any future email campaign.

<Info>
  Follow-up suggestions after a generation can also prompt you to save — for example, updating the original template versus saving as new — as this part of the experience continues to roll out.
</Info>

## Review and Apply the Email

Above the generated preview, use:

* The **eye icon** to toggle the rendered preview.
* The **`</>` icon** to switch to the raw HTML view — this shows the full markup with line numbers, including responsive CSS for mobile widths, dark mode support (`color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags), and Outlook-compatible markup (MSO conditional comments and XML namespaces).
* The **download icon** to export the HTML shown in the code view.

When you're ready to finish:

1. Use the **thumbs up / thumbs down** icons below the preview to rate the version. Clicking **thumbs down** opens a **Feedback** dialog with an optional **"Tell us more"** text field — add details and click **Submit**, or **Cancel** to skip it.
2. Click **Apply to campaign** to carry this version into your campaign's content step, or **Cancel** to discard it and return without applying anything.

# Guidelines to Write Effective Briefs

<Info>
  * Merlin AI Email Designer generates the best results from well-specified, single-turn briefs.
  * Include the campaign type, offer, and tone wherever possible.
  * If your brief is missing information Merlin AI Email Designer needs, it will ask you for it rather than guessing.
</Info>

## Brief Structure

Apply the following structure for the best results:

Create a `<campaign type>` email for `<product or collection>`, `<offer or detail>`, `<tone>`.

For example:

* Create a 48-hour flash sale campaign for our Summer Footwear collection. Offer 30% off to all users. Use an urgent tone and make sure the CTA stands out.
* Create a warm, friendly onboarding email welcoming new members to our loyalty programme.
* Make a spin wheel template similar to the given image.

## Common Campaign Types

The chat's starter options cover the most common campaign types: **Festival discount**, **Newsletter**, **Event invitation**, and **Welcome email**. Merlin AI Email Designer also recognizes other common types from a written brief without additional clarification, including abandoned cart flows, flash sales, and loyalty emails.

# Compliance and Guardrails

Merlin AI Email Designer applies compliance checks automatically and will not generate or publish content that violates them:

* Unsubscribe links cannot be removed. Merlin AI Email Designer declines the request, cites the relevant regulation (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, or the DPDP Act), and offers a compliant alternative where one exists.
* Deceptive subject lines, MENA-restricted content, content targeting minors, unverified financial or medical claims, and tracking scripts are declined.
* Publishing a campaign to the Push channel through Merlin AI Email Designer is blocked.

<Info>
  * Figma-to-HTML generation is not currently supported.
  * Mobile preview inside the designer isn't available yet.
  * Always review generated HTML in the preview pane before publishing, particularly personalisation tokens and locale content.
</Info>
