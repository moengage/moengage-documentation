## Explanation

The _Email Dropped_ event is an internal MoEngage event recorded only for smart-trigger (ST) and Flow email campaigns. MoEngage generates it when it evaluates business-use-case (BUC) checks at the point of delivery and drops the send because:

1. The user's unsubscribe, spam, or bounce attribute is true.
2. The user's email address is missing, or the email attribute specified in the campaign is invalid.

<Info>
  The _Email Dropped_ event is specific to MoEngage. Adding it to Flow campaigns significantly increased the overall _Email Dropped_ event count.
</Info>

## Why One-Time and Periodic Campaigns Show 0

BUC evaluation runs only during ST and Flow delivery, so one-time and periodic email campaigns do not generate the _Email Dropped_ event. A count of 0 for the _Email Dropped_ event on a one-time or periodic campaign is expected and is not a defect.
