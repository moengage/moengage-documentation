This page describes the standard **Email Open Tracking Consent** attribute (`moe_email_open_tracking_consent`) that MoEngage uses to decide whether to include the open tracking pixel for a recipient, along with the ways you can keep this attribute up to date.

## How the Consent Attribute Controls Tracking

For recipients whose country matches one of your regulated countries (or when you've selected **Use consent attribute** for missing-country cases), MoEngage checks this attribute to decide whether to include the tracking pixel:

- **Opted in:** The tracking pixel is included in their email, and opens continue to be tracked against their profile as usual.
- **Opted out** or **not present:** The tracking pixel is not included for that recipient's email.

Every time this attribute is updated, MoEngage raises an event named **Email Open Tracking Consent Updated** on the user's profile, giving you a record of when and how a recipient's consent status changed.

<Warning>
  In the absence of an explicit opt-in, a recipient is always treated as opted out. This is a regulatory requirement — consent cannot be assumed or defaulted to "yes." Make sure this attribute is being updated through one of the supported paths below before you rely on it.
</Warning>

## Updating the Consent Attribute

You can update this attribute using:

- User Import
- MoEngage SDK
- Server-to-Server (S2S) Data API
- The consent management page, where recipients can set their own preference

## Consent Management Link and Page

Once open tracking consent is enabled, MoEngage automatically inserts a consent management link in the footer of emails sent to recipients in your regulated countries. This link is separate from your unsubscribe link — recipients who use it to opt out of tracking will continue to receive your emails as normal; they just won't be tracked for opens.

The link takes recipients to a MoEngage-hosted consent page where they can view and update their open tracking preference. In this release, the page is unbranded and available in English only, and there's no support for using a custom or self-hosted consent page.
