To create Facebook Audience campaigns in MoEngage, you need to connect your MoEngage account with your Meta app and Meta ad account. This setup allows you to sync user audiences from MoEngage directly to Facebook, enabling targeted advertising based on your user segments.

This article explains how to set up or use an existing Meta app and a Meta ad account. Before you begin, understand the necessary permissions for Meta apps.

<Info>
  **Required Meta App Permissions**

  The MoEngage integration with Facebook Custom Audiences requires **standard access** to the **`ads_management`** and **`ads_read`** permissions. Without these permissions, MoEngage cannot add or remove users from your Facebook Custom Audiences.

  - If your app manages **your own ad account**, standard access to `ads_management` and `ads_read` is sufficient. Adding or removing users from your Facebook Custom Audiences does not require advanced access.
  - You need **advanced access** only if your app manages ad accounts that belong to **other businesses**. Advanced access requires Meta's [App Review](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-review/introduction) process.

  When you add a **Marketing API use case** to your app (described below), these permissions are added automatically.

## Set up your Meta app

<Note>
  Meta no longer uses app types (Business, Consumer, Gaming, None). App creation is now based on **use cases**, which automatically add the permissions, features, and products your app needs. You must be logged in to a registered Meta developer account to create an app.
</Note>

    1. Go to [developers.facebook.com/apps/creation](https://developers.facebook.com/apps/creation/) to start the app creation process.
    2. On the **App details** screen, enter your app name and a contact email address, and then click **Next**. The contact email is where Meta sends information about policies, app restrictions, or recovery if your app is deleted or compromised, so use an address you check regularly.

       {/* TODO(screenshot): Use cases selection screen */}
    4. On the **Business** screen, connect a business portfolio (verified or unverified). Connecting a business portfolio is recommended, because your ad account and custom audiences are managed within it. Click **Next**.

    2. If the app does not already have a Marketing API use case, add one so that the `ads_management` and `ads_read` permissions are available.

## Set up your Meta ad account

You add an ad account to your business portfolio in **Meta Business Suite**. You have three options: create a new ad account, claim an existing one, or request access to one in another business portfolio.

<Note>
  Once an ad account is added to a business portfolio, it is managed through that portfolio, and you assign people access from there. Permissions assigned at the portfolio level override asset-level permissions.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Create a New Ad Account">
    ### Create a new Meta ad account

    Creating an ad account in a business portfolio permanently adds it to that portfolio. The account cannot be deleted or transferred to another portfolio.

    1. In **Meta Business Suite**, go to **Settings**.
    2. In the left menu, select **Accounts**, and then select **Ad accounts**.
    3. Click the **+ Add** drop-down menu, and then select **Create a new ad account**.
       {/* TODO(screenshot): + Add > Create a new ad account */}
    4. Enter the **Ad account name**, **time zone**, and **currency**, and then click **Next**.
       {/* TODO(screenshot): New ad account details */}
    5. Choose the business portfolio you want to add the ad account to, and then select **My business**.
    6. Confirm that you want to create the account, select the checkbox to agree to the Meta Terms of Service and Advertising Standards, and then click **Create ad account**.
    7. Click **Add payment info** to add a payment method now, or click **Done** to add it later from the **Payment methods** tab in **Billing & payments**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Claim an Existing Ad Account">
    ### Claim an existing Meta ad account

    Claiming an existing ad account may permanently move it into your business portfolio. An ad account can only belong to one business portfolio at a time, and you must have full control of the portfolio.

    1. In **Meta Business Suite**, go to **Settings**.
    2. In the left menu, select **Accounts**, and then select **Ad accounts**.
    3. Click the **+ Add** drop-down menu, and then select **Add an ad account**.
       {/* TODO(screenshot): + Add > Add an ad account */}
    4. Enter the **Ad account ID**.
    5. Confirm that you want to claim the account, select the checkbox to agree to the Meta Terms of Service and Advertising Standards, and then click **Claim ad account**.

    If someone else owns the ad account, they must approve the request before you can use it. If you own it, no approval is required.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Request to Share an Ad Account">
    ### Request access to an ad account in another portfolio

    Use this option if your business needs to use an ad account that belongs to another business — for example, an agency working on a client's ad account. The account stays in the other portfolio, and access is shared with yours.

    1. In **Meta Business Suite**, go to **Settings**.
    2. In the left menu, select **Accounts**, and then select **Ad accounts**.
    3. Click **+ Add**, and then select **Request access to an ad account in another business portfolio**.
    4. Enter the **Ad account ID**.
    5. Choose the level of access to request — **partial access** or **full control**. If you request partial access, select the tasks you need.
    6. Click **Confirm** to send the request. You can use the ad account once the owner or someone with full control of its business portfolio approves your request.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
