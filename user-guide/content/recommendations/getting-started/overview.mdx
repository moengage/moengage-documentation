Recommendations let you show each customer the products, content, or offers they are most likely to act on — automatically, and at scale. Instead of promoting the same items to everyone, you can surface the right item at the right moment, whether that is a personalized pick, a relevant alternative, or a timely price-drop alert.

![MoEngage Recommendations engine overview](/images/moengage_78b355.png)

The Recommendations engine covers the full range, from promoting the same curated items to everyone, to identifying relevant items tailored to each individual, inspired by similar users' behavior or item similarities.

## How Recommendations Work

Setting up a recommendation follows four steps. You complete the first once, then reuse it across every recommendation you build.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your data">
    Connect an **item catalog** (your products, content, or listings) and **map your user-action events** (views, cart adds, wishlist saves, purchases) to MoEngage's standard events. See [Prerequisites](/user-guide/content/recommendations/prerequisites/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a recommendation type">
    Decide between **Basic** (rules you define) and **Advanced** (machine-learning models). See [Choosing a Model](#choosing-a-model) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and test">
    Configure the recommendation, then preview results for sample users before you launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a campaign">
    Insert the recommendation into a Push, Email, In-App, or other campaign to deliver personalized content to your users.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Basic vs Advanced Recommendations

MoEngage offers two families of recommendations. Most accounts use both, depending on the campaign.

| | **Basic** | **Advanced** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How it decides** | Rules you define | Machine-learning models (Merlin AI) |
| **Best when** | You know exactly what to promote | You want personalization to adapt automatically |
| **Effort** | You write the filters and sorting | The model learns from behavior |
| **Data readiness** | Catalog (and user actions for some models) | Catalog **plus** enough mapped user-action history to train |
| **Models** | Item Attributes, User Actions, Catalog Alerts | Recommended Items, Similar Items, Frequently Viewed Together, Frequently Bought Together, Trending Items, Popular Items |

## Choosing a Model

Start from your goal and work back to the model.

| I want to… | Use | Family |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promote specific products — new arrivals, a brand, seasonal picks | **Item Attributes** | Basic |
| Recall items a user showed intent on — cart, wishlist, recently viewed | **User Actions** | Basic |
| Alert users to price drops, back-in-stock, or fast-selling items | **Catalog Alerts** | Basic |
| Give each user personalized "recommended for you" picks | **Recommended Items** | Advanced |
| Show alternatives to an item a user is viewing | **Similar Items** | Advanced |
| Cross-sell items often viewed together | **Frequently Viewed Together** | Advanced |
| Bundle items often bought together | **Frequently Bought Together** | Advanced |
| Surface what's trending overall, or within a category or region | **Trending Items** (and **Popular Items** as a default fallback) | Advanced |

## Benefits

- **Higher engagement and conversion** — relevant suggestions keep users active and increase the likelihood of purchase.
- **Better cross-sell and upsell** — contextual bundles and add-ons lift average order value.
- **Reduced churn** — the right item at the right touchpoint keeps users coming back.
- **Stronger loyalty** — personalized experiences make customers feel understood.

## Recommendations Across Industries

Recommendations apply to almost any catalog-driven business. A few representative use cases:

| Industry | Example use case |
| --- | --- |
| Retail | Recommend complementary products, add-ons, or upgrades; re-engage on abandoned carts and wishlists. |
| Banking and Finance | Suggest loans, insurance, or investments based on a customer's profile; surface relevant card offers. |
| Classifieds and Real Estate | Recommend jobs matched to a seeker's skills, or listings near the user's location. |
| Ed-Tech | Recommend courses or certifications based on a learner's goals and interests. |
| Entertainment and Media | Recommend shows, articles, or videos based on viewing history and similar users. |
| Travel | Recommend packages or destinations based on traveler preferences and past trips. |
| Gaming | Suggest new titles based on genres and games a player has already played. |
