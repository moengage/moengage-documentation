# Introduction

[Snowflake](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide-getting-started) is a data platform that allows organizations to store, analyze, and process large volumes of structured and semi-structured data in a highly scalable and efficient manner. With its unique architecture, Snowflake allows organizations to consolidate their data, perform quick analytics, and gain valuable data-driven insights accessible to all users.

# MoEngage `<>` Snowflake

The MoEngage and Snowflake integration exports Campaign Interaction Events data directly to your Snowflake tables.

# Integration

<Tip>
  - Ensure you have a Snowflake account with permission to create/edit Snowflake instances, roles, and policies. 
  - Optional: To create a new user, you need to have access to create a new user in your Snowflake database. 
  - If your system requires IP whitelisting, refer to this [doc](https://moengage.com/docs/user-guide/settings/account/security/ip-whitelisting-in-moengage). 
  - Exports to Snowflake are part of the Streams add-on. To check what's available for your plan, refer to [Plans and Pricing](https://www.moengage.com/plans-and-pricing/). To enable it for your account, contact your MoEngage Customer Success Manager (CSM) or the Support team.
</Tip>

Read our [Snowflake](https://partners.moengage.com/hc/en-us/articles/17870012683028-Snowflake) guide to set up MoEngage `<>` Snowflake integration for your account.
