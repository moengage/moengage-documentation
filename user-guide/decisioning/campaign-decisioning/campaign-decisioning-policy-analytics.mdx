Policy analytics show how a Campaign Decisioning Policy performs across all campaigns that use that policy. Performance is measured as uplift, which is the difference between the policy treatment group and the policy control group.

- The policy treatment group includes end users who receive the policy campaigns.
- The policy control group includes end users held back as a baseline.

<Info>
MoEngage calculates and displays metrics based on the timezone configured in your app settings. Data updates automatically when you change the global filters.
</Info>

# Access Policy Analytics

Perform the following steps to access policy analytics.

1. On the left navigation menu in the MoEngage dashboard, click **Decisioning**, and then click **Campaign decisioning policy**.
2. From the list, click the policy that you want to analyze.
3. Click the **Analytics** tab.

The **Info** tab shows the policy configuration, including target, send limits, time slots, control group, and performance metrics.

# Filter Data

You can filter policy analytics by using the following options:

- Date range
- Attribution type, such as click-through attribution

# Policy Summary

The **Policy summary** cards provide a high-level view of policy impact compared with the control group. These cards show the following:

- Reward event uplift
- Brand engagement uplift
- Message interactions uplift
- Custom metrics uplift
- Campaigns sent
- Decisions made

Each uplift metric is shown as a percentage versus the policy control group.

# Policy Performance

The **Performance summary** table compares the policy treatment group with the policy control group.

The table contains the following columns:

- Metric
- Policy treatment group
- Policy control group
- % Uplift

Metrics are grouped under the following categories:

- Reward event
- Brand engagement
- Message interactions
- Custom metrics

Each group expands to show individual events.

The analytics page also contains the following sections:

- **Performance trend:** A chart of treatment versus control over time. You can filter the chart by event category, view it by Days, Weeks, or Months, and export it.
- **Policy campaigns:** A channel breakdown donut, a per-channel bar chart, and a searchable, filterable campaign performance table.

# Policy Performance Within a Campaign

Campaigns sent through a Campaign Decisioning Policy also show a **Policy performance** container in the individual campaign analytics, along with the standard campaign statistics.

This container shows the following:

- Policy name
- **View policy** link
- A table that compares the policy treatment group and policy control group for the same metric categories

This container does not appear for Standard campaigns.
