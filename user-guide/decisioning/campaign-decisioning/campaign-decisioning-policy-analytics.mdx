# Overview

Policy analytics show how a Campaign Decisioning Policy is performing across all its campaigns. Performance is measured as uplift — the difference between the **policy treatment group** (users who receive the policy's campaigns) and the **policy control group** (users held back as a baseline).

<Info>
MoEngage calculates and displays metrics based on the timezone configured in your app settings. Data updates automatically when you change the global filters.
</Info>

# Access Policy Analytics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the policy list">
    On the sidebar menu, click **Decisioning**, and then click **Campaign decisioning policy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a policy">
    From the list, click the policy you want to analyze.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Analytics tab">
    Click the **Analytics** tab. (The **Info** tab shows the policy's configuration — target, send limits, time slots, control group, and performance metrics.)
  </Step>
</Steps>

# Filter Your Data

Use the filters at the top of the Analytics tab:

- **Date range** — define the time period for the analysis.
- **Attribution type** — for example, click-through attribution.

# Policy Summary

The **Policy summary** cards give a high-level view of the policy's impact against the control group:

- **Reward event uplift**, **Brand engagement uplift**, **Message interactions uplift**, and **Custom metrics uplift** — each shown as a percentage versus the policy control group.
- **Campaigns sent** — the number of campaigns sent, out of the total policy campaigns.
- **Decisions made** — the total number of per-user decisions the policy has made.

{/* Screenshot: Policy summary uplift cards (Figma: Frame 1984078671). */}

# Policy Performance

The **Performance summary** table compares the policy treatment group against the policy control group:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Metric** | The metric or event, grouped by Reward event, Brand engagement, Message interactions, and Custom metrics. Each group expands to show individual events. |
| **Policy treatment group** | The value for users who received the policy's campaigns. |
| **Policy control group** | The value for the held-back baseline users. |
| **% Uplift from policy control group** | The net uplift of the treatment group over the control group. |

## Performance Trend

The **Performance trend** chart shows the policy treatment group versus the policy control group over time. You can filter by event category (Reward event, Brand engagement, Message interactions, Custom metrics), switch the granularity between **Days**, **Weeks**, and **Months**, and export the chart.

## Policy Campaigns

- **Channel breakdown** — a donut chart showing the distribution of the policy's campaigns across channels.
- **Channel performance** — a bar chart comparing the treatment and control groups per channel, filterable by event category.
- **Campaign performance** — a table of all campaigns under the policy, with columns such as Campaign name, Channel, Published on, Sent, Delivered, Clicks, and reward/engagement metrics. The table is searchable and filterable by channel and publish date.

# Policy Performance Within a Campaign

Campaigns sent through a Campaign Decisioning Policy also show a **Policy performance** container inside the individual campaign's analytics, alongside the standard campaign stats. The container shows the policy name, a **View policy** link, and a table comparing the policy treatment group and policy control group for the same metric categories. This container does not appear for Standard campaigns.
