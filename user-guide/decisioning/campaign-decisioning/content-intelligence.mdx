# Overview

Content Intelligence automatically labels the message variations in your campaign against your [Content Labels](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/content-labels) taxonomy. These labels give AI Campaign Decisioning the signal it needs to learn which content each user prefers and to deliver the variation most likely to convert.

Content Intelligence does three things:

- **Auto-labeling** — MoEngage analyzes each variation and assigns labels automatically.
- **Editable labels** — you can review any generated label and add or remove labels on a variation.
- **Regenerates on edit** — if you change a variation's copy, the labels are regenerated to match the new content.

<Info>
Labels are defined in [Content Labels](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/content-labels) and shared across your workspace. Labels are generated from message text only — image-only templates generate no labels.
</Info>

# Prerequisites

- AI Campaign Decisioning is enabled for your account.
- Your [Content Labels](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/content-labels) taxonomy is set up.
- You are creating a Push campaign with the **AI Campaign Decisioning** delivery strategy and have added more than one variation. For the full campaign flow, see [Create a Campaign with AI Campaign Decisioning](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/create-a-campaign-with-campaign-decisioning).

# Open Content Intelligence

In **Step 2: Content** of the campaign, once you add more than one variation, click **Content intelligence** in the upper-right corner. Auto-labeling is required for AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns.

{/* Screenshot: Step 2 Content with the "Content intelligence" link and the intro modal (Figma: Content intelligence with auto labelling). */}

# Review and Edit Labels

Content Intelligence opens a grid of your message variations, each shown with its locale, variation number, message content, and generated labels.

- **Filter** by Locales, Labels, and Platform, or **search** by locale, label, or variation.
- Toggle **Show images** to show or hide creatives, and use **Sort** to reorder the list.
- On any variation card, click a label's **×** to remove it, or click **+ Label** to add one. Changes apply to that variation only.
- The count of **unsaved changes** is shown at the bottom of the grid.

{/* Screenshot: Content Intelligence variation grid with label chips, filters, and "N unsaved changes" (Figma: Content intelligence grid). */}

# Edit a Variation's Copy

To change a variation's message, hover over the card and click **Edit**. MoEngage takes you back to **Step 2** to edit the content, and your label mapping changes are applied automatically.

When you edit a variation's title or message, the labels for that variation are regenerated and any manual labels on it are overwritten. Regenerated variations are flagged so you can review them with the **Show changed** filter.

# Save Labels

Click **Continue** to save the variations and their labels and proceed with the campaign.

<Note>
Auto-labeling is required for AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns and does not apply to Standard campaigns.
</Note>
