Content Intelligence automatically labels the message variations in your campaign against your Content Labels taxonomy. These labels give AI Campaign Decisioning the signal that it needs to learn which content each end user prefers and to deliver the variation most likely to convert.

Content Intelligence includes the following:

- **Auto-labeling:** MoEngage analyzes each variation and assigns labels automatically.
- **Editable labels:** You can review any generated label and add or remove labels on a variation.
- **Regenerates on edit:** If you change a variation's copy, the labels are regenerated to match the new content.

<Info>
Labels are defined in Content Labels and shared across your workspace. Labels are generated from message text only. Image-only templates generate no labels.
</Info>

# Prerequisites

Before you use Content Intelligence, make sure that the following conditions are met:

- AI Campaign Decisioning is enabled for your workspace.
- Your Content Labels taxonomy is set up.
- You are creating a Push campaign with the AI Campaign Decisioning delivery strategy.
- You have added more than one variation.

# Open Content Intelligence

In Step 2 of the campaign, after you add more than one variation, click **Content intelligence** in the upper-right corner.

Auto-labeling is required for AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns.

# Review and Edit Labels

Content Intelligence opens a grid of message variations. Each card shows the locale, variation number, message content, and generated labels.

You can perform the following actions:

- Filter by **Locales**, **Labels**, and **Platform**.
- Search by variation, content, or label.
- Turn **Show images** on or off to show or hide creatives.
- Use **Sort** to reorder the list.
- On any variation card, click **×** on a label to remove it.
- On any variation card, click **+ Label** to add a label.

Changes apply only to that variation.

The number of unsaved changes is shown at the bottom of the grid.

{/* Screenshot: Variation grid with label chips, filters, and unsaved-changes count */}

# Edit a Variation's Copy

Perform the following steps to edit a variation from Content Intelligence.

1. Hover over the variation card.
2. Click **Edit**.

MoEngage returns you to Step 2 so that you can edit the variation content.

When you edit a variation title or message, the labels for that variation are regenerated, and any manual labels on that variation are overwritten. Regenerated variations are flagged so that you can review them with the **Show changed** filter.

# Save Labels

To save the labels and continue with the campaign, click **Continue**.

<Note>
Auto-labeling is required for AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns and does not apply to Standard campaigns.
</Note>
