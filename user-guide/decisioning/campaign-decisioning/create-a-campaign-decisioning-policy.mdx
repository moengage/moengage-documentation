# Overview

A Campaign Decisioning Policy defines what the AI optimizes for and the guardrails it works within. It holds the reward event, the performance metrics you track, who is eligible, how many messages a user can receive, and the control group used to measure impact.

This article guides you through creating a Campaign Decisioning Policy and managing existing policies.

<Info>
Users with the required policy permissions can create policies. You can also define a custom role with permissions to create and manage policies.
</Info>

# Terms to Know

| Term | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Reward event** | The single User Behavior Event the policy optimizes for, such as "Product purchased". |
| **Performance metrics** | Additional events tracked as positive or negative signals to measure how the policy is performing against the control group. |
| **Eligibility** | The audience the policy can send to. At send time, the policy audience is intersected with each campaign's audience. |
| **Policy control group** | A random sample of eligible users held back from the policy's campaigns, used as a baseline to measure the policy's impact. |

# Create a Policy

1. On the left navigation menu, click **Decisioning**, and then click **Campaign decisioning policy**.
2. On the **Campaign Decisioning Policy** page, click **+ Create policy**.

{/* Screenshot: Decisioning → Campaign decisioning policy, empty state with "Create policy" (Figma: Create AI decisioning policy to get started). */}

Creating a policy has two steps: **Goals** and **Controls**.

## Step 1: Goals

Define what the policy optimizes for and how you measure it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the basic details">
    - **Policy name** (required).
    - **Purpose** (optional) — a short description of the policy. This description is shown to marketers when they select the policy during campaign creation.
    - **Reward event** (required) — select one User Behavior Event that defines success for this policy, such as "Product purchased". Each policy has exactly one reward event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the performance metrics">
    In the **Performance metrics** section, choose the signals that show whether the policy is working, measured against your control group. Metrics are split into **Positive metrics** and **Negative metrics** across these groups:

    - **Reward event** — mirrors the reward event you selected.
    - **Brand engagement** — includes **App/site opened** by default, which cannot be removed. You can add or remove other events.
    - **Message interactions** — for example, Notification clicked (iOS, Android, Web), Email opened, Email clicked, WhatsApp read, WhatsApp clicked, SMS clicked.
    - **Custom metrics** — your own events. You can add up to 3 manual metrics.

    The same event cannot appear as both positive and negative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Click **Next** to proceed to the Controls step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Controls

Define who receives messages from the policy and how many.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set eligibility and exclusion">
    Under **Eligibility and exclusion**, choose **All users** or **Filter users by** segment criteria, and optionally **Exclude users**. Click **Show count** to see the eligible audience size, or **Reset filters** to clear your criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set daily send limits">
    Under **Daily send limits**, set a minimum and maximum number of communications a user can receive per day. If this range exceeds your account's global frequency cap, MoEngage shows a warning with a link to frequency capping settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add time slots (optional)">
    Turn on **Add time slots** to restrict when the policy sends. For each slot, enter a **Name** and the **From** and **To** times, and optionally use **Set send limits** to add a per-slot minimum and maximum. Overlapping slots are not allowed, and a slot's minimum cannot exceed the policy maximum. The **Slots preview** shows your slots on a 24-hour timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the policy control group">
    Under **Policy control group**, set the percentage of eligible users (default 10%) held back from the policy's campaigns to measure performance impact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the policy">
    Click **Save policy**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# Manage Your Policies

The **Campaign Decisioning Policy** page lists all policies you have created, with their status, last updated date, active campaigns, decisions made, and policy performance. On this page, you can:

- **Search and filter** — use the **Search by policy name** box or the **Select status** list.
- **Manage** — use the ellipsis (⋮) menu on a policy row for the available actions.
- **Edit** — open a policy to modify its configuration.
