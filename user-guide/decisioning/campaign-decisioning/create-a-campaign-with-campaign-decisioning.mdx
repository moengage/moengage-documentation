# Overview

With AI Campaign Decisioning, you create a single Push campaign with several complete message variations, let MoEngage auto-label them, and let a Campaign Decisioning Policy pick the best variation for each user.

<Warning>
- Create the [Campaign Decisioning Policy](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/create-a-campaign-decisioning-policy) you intend to use before you attach it to a campaign.
- AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns are computed ahead of time. Schedule your campaign at least 24 hours in advance.
- Set up your [Content Labels](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/content-labels) taxonomy before onboarding, so message variations can be labeled accurately.
</Warning>

# Create the Campaign

1. On the sidebar menu, hover over the **Engage** icon and click **+ New Campaign**. You can also open **All Campaigns** and click **+ Create campaign**.
2. Select the **Push** channel and a delivery type (**One Time** or **Periodic**).

{/* Screenshot: New Campaign menu → Push (Figma: Frame 9618). */}

The process has three steps: **Target users**, **Content**, and **Schedule and goals**.

## Step 1: Target Users

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the campaign details">
    Select a **Team**, enter a **Campaign name**, and optionally add **Campaign tags**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose target platforms">
    Select **Android**, **iOS**, or both, and configure any platform-specific push settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the delivery strategy">
    In the **Delivery strategy** section, select one of:

    - **Standard** — the user receives all campaigns they are eligible for, based on your targeting and capping rules.
    - **AI Campaign Decisioning** (Powered by Aampe) — when a user qualifies for multiple campaigns, the AI automatically chooses the campaigns and content the user is most likely to engage with.

    When you choose AI Campaign Decisioning, select the **policy that manages this campaign** from the list of active policies. Each policy shows its purpose description to help you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the target audience">
    Choose **All users** or **Filter users by** segment criteria. The campaign audience is combined with the policy audience at send time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* Screenshot: Step 1 Target users → Delivery strategy (Standard vs AI Campaign Decisioning) with "Select policy that manages this campaign" (Figma: Step 1 - No change). */}

<Note>
If you switch a campaign from AI Campaign Decisioning back to Standard after adding content, MoEngage warns you that the content labels and AI scheduling info added in later steps will be removed.
</Note>

## Step 2: Content

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add message variations">
    Use **+ Variation** to add complete Push message variations (title, message, and other fields), and **+ Locale** to add locale variants. You can add up to 150 variations across locales and platforms. Use **Copy content** or **New variation** to speed up authoring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Auto-label with Content Intelligence">
    Once you add more than one variation, click **Content intelligence** to auto-label your variations and review the labels. Auto-labeling is required for AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns. For details, see [Content Intelligence](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/content-intelligence).
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* Screenshot: Step 2 Content with variation tabs, locales, and the Content intelligence link (Figma: Option A content step). */}

## Step 3: Schedule and Goals

<Note>
AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns use fixed-time scheduling in Phase 0. Schedule your campaign at least 24 hours in advance so the AI can prepare its predictions.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Schedule the campaign">
    Set a fixed date and time to send. Both one-time and periodic (daily, weekly, monthly) campaigns are supported. The **Policy time slots** panel shows the windows within which the policy can send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set conversion goals">
    Add campaign-level conversion goals to track for reporting, if applicable. AI optimization uses the reward event and performance metrics configured in the selected policy, not these campaign goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# FAQ

<Accordion title="Why must campaigns be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance?">
  AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns are pre-computed. The policy needs this lead time to analyze user data and prepare per-user decisions, so same-day sends will not have AI-optimized delivery.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does per-user content selection work?">
  AI Campaign Decisioning first identifies the best campaign for each user, then selects the specific content variation most likely to maximize the policy's reward, based on the labels applied in Content Intelligence.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does AI Campaign Decisioning support locales?">
  Yes. You add locale variants with **+ Locale** in Step 2, and the correct locale is delivered based on your locale setup.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if the global frequency cap is stricter than the policy?">
  The global frequency cap always applies on top of the policy's send limits, so some AI-selected messages may not be sent. This is expected behavior.
</Accordion>
