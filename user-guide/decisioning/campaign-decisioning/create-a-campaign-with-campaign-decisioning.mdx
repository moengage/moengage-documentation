<Note>
**Early Access**

AI Campaign Decisioning is available only for Push. To enable it for your workspace, contact your MoEngage Customer Success Manager (CSM) or the Support team.
</Note>

With AI Campaign Decisioning, you create one Push campaign with several complete message variations, allow MoEngage to auto-label them, and let a Campaign Decisioning Policy select the best variation for each end user.

**Before you start:**

- Create the Campaign Decisioning Policy that you intend to use.
- Schedule the campaign at least 24 hours in advance because AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns are computed ahead of time.
- Set up your Content Labels taxonomy so that message variations can be labeled accurately.

# Step 1: Target Users

Perform the following steps to configure campaign targeting.

1. Enter the campaign details:
   - Select a **Team**.
   - Enter a **Campaign name**.
   - Optional: Add **Campaign tags**.
2. Select the target platforms:
   - **Android**
   - **iOS**
   - or both
3. In the **Delivery strategy** section, select one of the following options:
   - **Standard**. The end user receives all campaigns for which they are eligible, based on your targeting and capping rules.
   - **AI Campaign Decisioning (Powered by Aampe)**. When an end user qualifies for multiple campaigns, the AI automatically selects the campaigns and content that the end user is most likely to engage with.

   <img src="/images/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/campaign-delivery-strategy.png" alt="The campaign Step 1 Delivery strategy section with the Standard and AI Campaign Decisioning (Powered by Aampe) options." />

4. If you select **AI Campaign Decisioning (Powered by Aampe)**, select the policy that manages the campaign. Each policy shows its purpose description to help you select the correct policy.
5. Select the target audience:
   - **All users**
   - or **Filter users by segment criteria**

At send time, the campaign reaches only end users who are in both the campaign audience and the policy audience.

<Note>
If you switch a campaign from AI Campaign Decisioning to Standard after you add content, MoEngage displays a warning that the content labels and AI scheduling information added in later steps are removed.
</Note>

# Step 2: Content

Perform the following steps to add campaign content.

1. Add message variations:
   - Use **+ Variation** to add complete Push message variations.
   - Use **+ Locale** to add locale variants.
   - You can add up to 150 variations across locales and platforms.

   <img src="/images/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/campaign-content.png" alt="The campaign Content step with the message editor, plus + Variation and + Locale controls." />

2. After you add more than one variation, click **Content intelligence** to auto-label the variations and review the labels.

Auto-labeling is required for AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns.

# Step 3: Schedule and Goals

<Note>
AI Campaign Decisioning campaigns use fixed-time scheduling. Schedule your campaign at least 24 hours in advance so that the AI can prepare its predictions.
</Note>

Perform the following steps to configure scheduling and goals.

1. Schedule the campaign:
   - Set a fixed date and time to send.
   - One-time and periodic campaigns are supported.
   - Periodic campaigns can be daily, weekly, or monthly.
   - The **Policy time slots** panel shows the windows within which the policy can send.

   <img src="/images/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/campaign-schedule.png" alt="The campaign Schedule and goals step with a fixed date and time and campaign time zone." />

2. Set conversion goals:
   - Optional: Add campaign-level conversion goals for reporting.
   - AI optimization uses the reward event and performance metrics configured in the selected policy, not these campaign goals.
3. Click **Publish**.
