# Overview

AI Campaign Decisioning, powered by Aampe, determines the best message copy for each individual user in your Push campaigns. A Campaign Decisioning Policy holds the goal and guardrails, and the AI learns across campaigns which content works for whom, then delivers the variation most likely to convert for each user.

# Policy Components

Every policy is built on the following foundations:

- **The reward**: Each policy has one reward event that defines success (for example, "Product purchased"). The policy also tracks additional **performance metrics** as positive or negative signals, so you can measure impact beyond the reward event alone.
- **Guardrails**: You set the boundaries the AI works within — the eligible audience, the daily send limits (minimum and maximum), optional time slots, and the policy control group. The AI optimizes strictly within these boundaries, and your account's global frequency cap always applies on top of them.

# How Content Is Chosen

- You write several complete message variations for a campaign.
- [Content Intelligence](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/content-intelligence) auto-labels each variation against your [Content Labels](/user-guide/decisioning/campaign-decisioning/content-labels) taxonomy.
- The policy learns which labels each user responds to and delivers the variation most likely to convert for that user.

# How Guardrails Decide What Is Sent

Even after the AI selects the best variation for a user, policy guardrails and the global frequency cap determine whether the message can be sent:

1. **Policy limits** — the policy's maximum sends per user, plus any time-slot limits.
2. **Global frequency cap** — your account-level limit on messages per user.

Some AI-selected messages are not sent because the global frequency cap takes precedence. This is expected behavior.

# Your Role

The AI handles scale, but it relies on you to steer it:

- **Strategic intent**: Define the reward event and performance metrics that represent your business goals.
- **Governance**: Set the audience, send limits, time slots, and control group that keep sending within your brand's rules.
- **Creative input**: Provide a diverse set of complete message variations so the AI has strong copy to choose from for each user.
