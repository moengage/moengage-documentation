This page describes what you need before you start, how to install the app, and how to sign in for the first time.

## Prerequisites

To use the app, you need:

- An active MoEngage account with access to the web dashboard.
- Permission to view the campaigns you want to monitor. The app shows the same campaigns and data your account can access on the web.
- If you approve campaigns, the campaign approval flow must be enabled for your team on the web dashboard.
- If you want to receive alerts, the relevant alerts must be configured on the web dashboard.

## Install the app

The app is available for iOS and Android.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your app store">
    On your device, open the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the app">
    Search for the MoEngage mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install and open">
    Install the app and open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in

The mobile login flow mirrors the web dashboard sign-in, including two-factor authentication (2FA).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Launch the app on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter the same email and password you use for the MoEngage web dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete two-factor authentication">
    If 2FA is enabled for your account, complete the verification step to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on the Home screen">
    After you sign in, the app opens to the Home screen for your default workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you cannot sign in, confirm your access on the web dashboard first.

## Enable push notifications

To receive alerts and approval requests on your device, allow notifications when the app prompts you. If you skip this step, you can enable notifications later in your device settings for the app. Without notifications enabled, alerts and approval requests still appear in the Notifications tab, but you will not be notified when they arrive.
