MoEngage authenticates your identity separately from your access to individual workspaces. As a result, an access issue with one workspace — for example, a revoked SSO account or a workspace-level policy change — does not affect your access to other workspaces you're authorized to use. Depending on how you reach MoEngage, you log in directly to your last-used workspace, or verify your identity once and then select from the workspaces you have access to.

<Tip>
  **Prerequisites**

  - **First login (as an invited user)**: Complete a one-time setup before you log in for the first time after being invited to a workspace. For more information, refer to [Finish Your Workspace Setup as an Invited User](/user-guide/getting-started/initial-setup/sign-up-with-moengage-or-create-a-new-account-in-moengage#finish-your-workspace-setup-as-an-invited-user).
  - **Subsequent logins**: Keep your workspace login page URL handy. If you don't remember it, see [Find Your Login Page](#find-your-login-page) below.
  - **2-step verification**: If your workspace requires 2-step verification, set it up on your device before you log in. For more information, refer to [Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device](#set-up-2-step-verification-on-your-device).
</Tip>

## Find Your Login Page

MoEngage hosts each workspace in a specific data center for performance and data-residency compliance. Your login URL includes the data center identifier — for example, `dashboard-01.moengage.com` for DC-01. If you don't know your login URL, use the MoEngage global login page to find it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the global login page">
    Go to [dashboard.moengage.com](https://dashboard.moengage.com/v4/#/auth).
    ![MoEngage global login page](/images/login1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your work email">
    In the **Work email** box, type your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to your data center">
    Click **Continue**. MoEngage redirects you to your last-used workspace's login page on the correct data center.
    ![Data center login page](/images/loginpage1.png)

    <Info>
      Your email address is pre-filled on the login page for verification.
    </Info>

    <Info>
      If your last-used workspace has Single Sign-On (SSO) enabled, MoEngage redirects you automatically to that data center's Login with Single Sign-On page.
      ![SSO redirect page](/images/moengage_ad225e.png)
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Want to reach a workspace other than your last-used one instead? Click **Login to a different workspace** on either page. MoEngage verifies your identity with a 2-step verification code, then lets you choose which workspace and environment to access. For more information, refer to [Log Into a Different Workspace](#log-into-a-different-workspace).

<Info>
  Landed on the wrong data center's login page? Click **Find your data centre** to return to the global login page and re-enter your email address.
</Info>

Bookmark your login URL for faster access in the future. For more information on data centers, refer to [Data Centers in MoEngage](/user-guide/data/key-concepts/data-centers-in-moengage).

## Log In to Your Last-Used Workspace

Logging in from your workspace's data center login page takes you directly to your last-used workspace — the fastest path if you mainly work in one workspace. If you're logging in to MoEngage for the first time, MoEngage takes you to your invited workspace instead.

Your MoEngage Admin configures which of the following login methods your workspace supports. For more information, refer to [Set Up the Login Method](/user-guide/settings/account/security/set-up-the-login-method).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Password">
    <Info>
      If your Admin set the login method to **Password or Google sign in**, you can log in with either your email and password or the **Google** button.
    </Info>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your workspace login page">
        In a web browser, go to the URL of your MoEngage workspace. The login page appears. <img src="/images/webbrowserlogin.png" alt="Login page" />
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your email">
        In the **Work email** box, type your email address.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your password">
        In the **Password** box, type your password.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Log in">
        Click **Login**. 2-step verification is mandatory when you log in with a password — see [Complete 2-Step Verification](#complete-2-step-verification) below.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Google">
    <Info>
      If your Admin set the login method to **Google Sign In only**, you must use the **Google** option — you cannot log in with a password.
    </Info>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your workspace login page">
        In a web browser, go to the URL of your MoEngage workspace. The login page appears. <img src="/images/webbrowserlogin.png" alt="Login page" />
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Google">
        Click **Google**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose your Google account">
        On the **Choose an account** page, choose your Google account. <img src="/images/moengage_889824.png" alt="Choose a Google account" />
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm sign-in">
        On the **Sign in to moengage.com** page, click **Continue**. <img src="/images/moengage_c8a1fa.png" alt="Sign in to moengage.com" /> If 2-step verification is enabled for your workspace, see [Complete 2-Step Verification](#complete-2-step-verification) below.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="SSO">
    <Info>
      If your Admin set the login method to **Single Sign On (SSO) only**, you must log in using SSO — you cannot use a password or the **Google** option.
    </Info>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your workspace login page">
        In a web browser, go to the URL of your MoEngage workspace. The login page appears.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click SSO">
        Click **SSO**. <img src="/images/loginviasso.png" alt="SSO" /> The Login with Single Sign On page appears, with your email pre-filled if you entered it on the previous screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your email">
        In the **Work Email** box, type your email address.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Continue to your identity provider">
        Click **Continue**. <img src="/images/moengage_205889.png" alt="SSO login page" /> MoEngage redirects you to your organization's Identity Provider (IdP) for authentication.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authenticate with your IdP">
        Log in to the SSO IdP using your credentials.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the MoEngage app">
        In the SSO IdP dashboard, click the MoEngage app. If 2-step verification is enabled for your workspace, see [Complete 2-Step Verification](#complete-2-step-verification) below.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Log Into a Different Workspace

Use this flow to log in to any workspace you're authorized to use, not only your last-used workspace — relevant if you manage multiple workspaces, such as across different brands or client accounts. MoEngage verifies your identity first, then your access to the selected workspace, so an access issue with one workspace does not prevent you from logging in to the others.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a different workspace">
    On the login page (or at [dashboard.moengage.com](https://dashboard.moengage.com/v4/#/auth)), click **Login to a different workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    In the **Work email** box, type your email address. If you entered it on the previous page, it's pre-filled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">
    Click **Continue**. The **User Authentication** page appears.

    ![Log in to a different workspace](/images/logintodiffws.gif)

    <Info>
      User Authentication uses the same 6-digit TOTP code as 2-step verification. MoEngage verifies your identity at this step — before listing your workspaces — because workspace credentials (password, Google, SSO) are workspace-specific and can't identify you across all of them. For information on setting up your authenticator app, refer to [Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device](#set-up-2-step-verification-on-your-device).
    </Info>

    Open your authenticator app, find the 6-digit code for your MoEngage account, and type it in the verification box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a workspace and environment">
    Click **Continue**. The **Select Workspace** page appears. Choose the workspace and environment you want to access. Each entry shows:

    - Workspace name
    - Whether it's your last-used workspace
    - Login method
    - Environment (Live or Test)

    ![Select workspace and log in](/images/logindiffws2.gif)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete workspace authentication">
    Complete authentication using the login method configured for the selected workspace:

    - **Password**: Enter your password and click **Login**.
    - **Google Sign-In**: Click **Google** and choose your Google account.
    - **SSO**: Click **SSO** to be redirected to your organization's Identity Provider (IdP).
  </Step>
</Steps>

MoEngage logs you in to the selected workspace.

## Log In via MCP Connection

The MoEngage MCP (MoEngage Connection Protocol) server lets AI assistants, such as Claude and ChatGPT, work with your campaigns directly. For more information on connecting AI assistants to MoEngage, refer to [MoEngage MCP Server and Connector](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/moengage-mcp-server#authentication). MCP connections switch between workspaces more frequently than dashboard sessions, so every MCP connection starts with the full workspace list instead of defaulting to your last-used workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initiate a connection">
    In your MCP client, initiate a new MoEngage connection. MoEngage redirects you to the global login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    In the **Work email** box, type your email address and click **Continue**. MoEngage redirects you to the login page for your last-used workspace's data center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">
    On the **User Authentication** page, open your authenticator app, find the 6-digit code for MoEngage, and type it in the verification box. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a workspace">
    On the **Select Workspace** page, choose the workspace and environment you want to connect to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete workspace authentication">
    Complete authentication using the workspace's configured login method (password, Google, or SSO).
  </Step>
</Steps>

MoEngage establishes your MCP connection.

<Info>
  MCP sessions last up to 30 days (expiry), with a 7-day idle timeout — longer than a standard dashboard session, since MCP clients typically stay connected across many work sessions. MCP sessions are independent from your MoEngage Dashboard session: logging in to a workspace via MCP doesn't create or affect an active Dashboard session, and vice versa.

  If your connection attempt is interrupted for any reason (password not set, 2-step verification not configured, network issues), initiate the connection again.
</Info>

## Complete 2-Step Verification

During the last-used workspace login flow, if 2-step verification is enabled, MoEngage shows the 2-Step Verification page after you authenticate.

- **If you haven't configured 2-step verification on your device yet**, the page prompts you to set it up. For more information, refer to [Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device](#set-up-2-step-verification-on-your-device). <img src="/images/moengage_943be8.png" alt="2-step verification setup prompt" />
- **If you've already configured 2-step verification**, enter the code from your authenticator app: <img src="/images/moengage_7fbf4b.png" alt="2-step verification code entry" />

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Get your code">
      Open the authenticator app on your device and find the 6-digit code for your MoEngage workspace.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Enter your code">
      On the 2-Step Verification page, type the code in the **Verification code** boxes.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify">
      Click **Verify**. MoEngage logs you in to the dashboard.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

<Tip>
  Can't complete 2-step verification — for example, you've lost your device or run out of recovery codes? See [Unable to Log In](#unable-to-log-in).
</Tip>

<Info>
  In the [Log Into a Different Workspace](#log-into-a-different-workspace) flow, the equivalent step is called **User Authentication** and happens before workspace selection, not after.
</Info>

## Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device

2-step verification requires a code from an authenticator app in addition to your regular credentials. Set it up once; your authenticator app then generates a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds for you to use at each login.

<Tip>
  **Prerequisites**

  You need an authenticator app on your phone (for example, Google Authenticator).

  - Google Authenticator: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.authenticator2&hl=en_IN&gl=US) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-authenticator/id388497605)
  - Twilio Authy: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.authy.authy) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/twilio-authy/id494168017)
</Tip>

### When You're Prompted to Set Up 2-Step Verification

MoEngage prompts you to set up 2-step verification in these scenarios:

- Your admin signs up for MoEngage or creates a new workspace — MoEngage enables 2-step verification by default.
- You're invited to a workspace that has 2-step verification enabled. After [finishing your workspace setup](/user-guide/getting-started/initial-setup/sign-up-with-moengage-or-create-a-new-account-in-moengage#finish-your-workspace-setup-as-an-invited-user), MoEngage prompts you to set it up.
- Your admin enables 2-step verification after you've already logged in. MoEngage logs you out automatically and prompts you on your next login.
- You enable 2-step verification for your own account in **My Profile**.

In each scenario, you land on the 2-Step Verification page:

![2-Step Verification page](/images/moengage_3a7ace.png)

### Configure 2-Step Verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start setup">
    On the 2-Step Verification page, click **Setup your device**.
    ![Setup your device button](/images/moengage_3a7ace.png)
    The Set your device authentication page appears.
    ![Set your device authentication page](/images/moengage_8c9228.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your authenticator app">
    Open your authenticator app and tap the **+** icon.
    <img src="/images/moengage_2e8e23.png" style={{ width:"35%" }} alt="Authenticator plus icon" />
    The Add an authentication code screen appears.
    <img src="/images/moengage_2d1624.png" style={{ width:"36%" }} alt="Add authentication code screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add MoEngage to your authenticator app">
    Use either option to add MoEngage:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="QR code">
        1. Tap **Scan a QR code**.
           <img src="/images/moengage_9ecee6.png" style={{ width:"35%" }} alt="Scan a QR code option" />
        2. Scan the QR code shown on the **Set your device authentication** page. Your authenticator app adds MoEngage and begins generating 6-digit codes.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Setup key">
        1. Tap **Enter a setup key**.
           <img src="/images/moengage_e6d745.png" style={{ width:"36%" }} alt="Enter a setup key option" />
        2. On the **Enter account details** screen, fill in the following fields:

           | Field | Required | Description |
           | --- | --- | --- |
           | Account name | Optional | Type your account name. |
           | Your key | Yes | Type the 32-digit secret key shown on the **Set your device authentication** page. <br /><img alt="Setup key" src="/images/moengage_331114.png" /> |
           | Type of key | Optional | **Time based** (OTP from current time) or **Counter based** (OTP from incrementing counter). |
           | <img src="/images/moengage_c59fc2.png" alt="Key type selection" /> |  |  |
        3. Tap **Add**. Your authenticator app adds MoEngage and begins generating 6-digit codes.
           <img src="/images/moengage_bf333b.png" style={{ width:"36%" }} alt="Authentication added" />
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the verification code">
    In the **Enter the 6 digit verification code** box, type the current code from your authenticator app.

    <Info>
      Each code is valid for 30 seconds. After that, your authenticator app generates a new code.
    </Info>

    ![Enter verification code](/images/moengage_1a5937.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm**. 2-step verification is now configured on your device.
    ![Setup success](/images/moengage_8d89ae.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your recovery codes">
    Click **Download recovery codes** to save your backup codes as a CSV file. For more information, refer to [Recovery Codes](#recovery-codes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Click **Login**. The Login to your accounts page appears.
    ![Login page after setup](/images/moengage_1a0416.png)
    [Log in to your workspace](#log-in-to-your-last-used-workspace).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Troubleshoot 2-Step Verification Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Codes are rejected or don't match">
    Make sure both your login device and the device with your authenticator app are set to the same timezone. Codes are time-based and expire after 30 seconds.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The login page won't load or behaves unexpectedly">
    Clear your browser's cookies and cache, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You're not sure you're on the right login page">
    Verify you're using the correct login URL for your workspace. Each workspace is hosted on a specific data center. For more information, refer to [Data Centers in MoEngage](/user-guide/data/key-concepts/data-centers-in-moengage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You didn't receive a reset email">
    Check that your organization hasn't blocked the MoEngage domain.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If the issue persists, [contact the MoEngage Support team](/user-guide/contact-support/raise-a-support-ticket-through-moengage-dashboard) or your Customer Success Manager (CSM).

## Recovery Codes

Recovery codes are a set of 10 one-time-use backup codes that let you log in to your MoEngage workspace when your primary 2-step verification device is unavailable. MoEngage generates these codes when you first set up or reset 2-step verification.

Store your recovery codes securely and don't share them. Each code works only once — MoEngage marks it invalid immediately after use.

If you use all your recovery codes, [reset 2-step verification](#reset-2-step-verification) to generate a new set.

## Reset 2-Step Verification

Reset 2-step verification when:

- You can't authenticate with the verification code from your current authenticator app — for example, you lost or replaced your device.
- You've used all of your recovery codes and need a new set.

<Tip>
  **Prerequisites**

  You must have previously set up 2-step verification on your device.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attempt to log in">
    Try to [log in to your workspace](#log-in-to-your-last-used-workspace) using your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a reset link">
    On the 2-Step Verification page, click **Receive reset 2-step verification link via email**. <img src="/images/moengage_d1089b.png" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    Click **Send link**. <img src="/images/moengage_60b241.png" alt="Send reset link" /> MoEngage sends a reset link to your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the reset link">
    Open the email from MoEngage and click **here to reset your 2FA credentials**. <img src="/images/moengage_b61f2f.png" /> You land on the Login to your accounts page in a new browser tab. <img src="/images/moengage_529598.png" alt="Login to your accounts page" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in again">
    Log in using your credentials. MoEngage takes you to the 2-Step Verification page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start setup on your new device">
    Click **Setup your device**. <img src="/images/moengage_2a04b3.png" /> The Set up your device authentication page appears. <img src="/images/moengage_d75795.png" alt="Set up device authentication" />

    Add MoEngage to your authenticator app using either a QR code or a setup key — the same two options described in [Configure 2-Step Verification](#configure-2-step-verification) above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the verification code">
    In the **Enter the 6 digit verification code** box, type the code from your authenticator app.

    <Info>
      Each code is valid for 30 seconds.
    </Info>

    ![Enter verification code](/images/moengage_7303d7.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm**. 2-step verification is now reset on your device — use the verification code from this new authenticator instance for all future logins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download new recovery codes">
    Click **Download recovery codes** to save a new set of backup codes as a CSV file. <img src="/images/moengage_6241c0.png" alt="Download recovery codes" /> Your previous recovery codes no longer work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Click **Login**. <img src="/images/moengage_ae816f.png" alt="Login page" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
