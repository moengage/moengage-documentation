  - **First login (as an invited user)**: You must complete a one-time setup before logging in for the first time after you are invited to a workspace. For more information, refer to [Finish Your Workspace Setup as an Invited User](/user-guide/getting-started/initial-setup/sign-up-with-moengage-or-create-a-new-account-in-moengage#finish-your-workspace-setup-as-an-invited-user).
  - **Subsequent logins**: If you have already finished your account setup and logged in to MoEngage, you must remember the login page URL. If you don't remember your workspace login URL, see [Find Your Login Page](#find-your-login-page) below.
  - **2-step verification**: If 2-step verification is enabled for your workspace, you must set it up on your device before logging in. For more information, refer to [Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device](#set-up-2-step-verification-on-your-device).
</Tip>

## Find Your Login Page

MoEngage hosts each workspace in a specific data center. Your login URL includes the data center identifier — for example, `dashboard-01.moengage.com` for DC-01. If you don't know your login URL, use the MoEngage global login page to find it:

1. Go to [dashboard.moengage.com](https://dashboard.moengage.com/v4/#/auth).
   ![MoEngage global login page](/images/moengage_d01571.png)
2. In the **Work email** box, type your email address.
3. Click **Continue**. MoEngage redirects you to your last used workspace's login page on the correct data center.
   ![Data center login page](/images/loginpage1.png)
   <Info>
     Your email address is pre-filled on the login page for verification.
   </Info>
   <Info>
     If Single Sign-On (SSO) is enabled on your last used workspace, you are automatically redirected to the correct DC's Login with Single Sign-On page.
     ![SSO redirect page](/images/moengage_ad225e.png)
   </Info>
4. Or click **Login to a different workspace** to log in to a workspace other than your last-used workspace. You are automatically redirected to your last used workspace's data centre where your identity is verified using a 2-step verification code, after which you can choose which workspace and environment to access. For more information, refer to [Log Into a Different Workspace](#log-into-a-different-workspace).

Bookmark your login URL for faster access in the future. For more information on data centers, refer to [Data Centers in MoEngage](/user-guide/data/key-concepts/data-centers-in-moengage).

## Log In to Your Last-Used Workspace

Logging in from your workspace's data center login page takes you directly to your last-used workspace. If you are logging into MoEngage for the first time, you are directed to your invited workspace instead.

You can log in using one of the following methods:

- [Using a Password](#using-a-password)
- [Using the Sign In with Google Option](#using-the-sign-in-with-google-option)
- [Using SSO](#using-sso)

### Using a Password

<Info>
  If your MoEngage Admin set up the login method as **Password or Google sign in**, you can log in using either your email and password or the **Sign in with Google** button. For more information, refer to [Set Up the Login Method](/user-guide/settings/account/security/set-up-the-login-method).
</Info>

1. In a web browser, go to the URL of your MoEngage workspace. The login page appears. <img src="/images/moengage_155710.png" alt="Login page" />
2. In the **Work email** box, type your email address.
3. In the **Password** box, type your password.
4. Click **Login**.
5. 2-step verification is mandatory if you are logging in using a password, see [Complete 2-Step Verification](#complete-2-step-verification) below.

### Using the Sign in with Google Option

<Info>
  If your MoEngage Admin set up the login method as **Google Sign In only**, you must use the **Sign in with Google** option. You cannot log in using a password. For more information, refer to [Set Up the Login Method](/user-guide/settings/account/security/set-up-the-login-method).
</Info>

1. In a web browser, go to the URL of your MoEngage workspace. The login page appears. <img src="/images/moengage_155710.png" alt="Login page" />
2. Click **Continue with Google**.
3. On the **Choose an account** page, choose your Google account. <img src="/images/moengage_889824.png" alt="Choose a Google account" />
4. On the **Sign in to moengage.com** page, click **Continue**. <img src="/images/moengage_c8a1fa.png" alt="Sign in to moengage.com" />
5. If 2-step verification is enabled for your workspace, see [Complete 2-Step Verification](#complete-2-step-verification) below.

### Using SSO

<Info>
  If your MoEngage Admin set up the login method as **Single Sign On (SSO) only**, you must log in using SSO. You cannot use a password or the **Sign in with Google** option. For more information, refer to [Set Up the Login Method](/user-guide/settings/account/security/set-up-the-login-method).
</Info>

1. In a web browser, go to the URL of your MoEngage workspace. The login page appears.
2. Click **Login using SSO**. <img src="/images/moengage_d00957.png" alt="Login using SSO" /> The Login with Single Sign On page appears. The email ID is pre-filled (if entered in the previous screen).
3. In the **Work Email** box, type your email address.
4. Click **Continue**. <img src="/images/moengage_205889.png" alt="SSO login page" /> You are redirected to your organization's Identity Provider (IdP) for authentication.
5. Log in to the SSO IdP using your credentials.
6. In the SSO IdP dashboard, click on the MoEngage app.
7. If 2-step verification is enabled for your workspace, see [Complete 2-Step Verification](#complete-2-step-verification) below.

## Log Into a Different Workspace

Use this flow to log in to a workspace other than your last-used workspace. MoEngage verifies your identity first using your 2-step verification code, then lets you choose which workspace and environment to access.

1. On the login page (or at [dashboard.moengage.com](https://dashboard.moengage.com/v4/#/auth)), click **Login to a different workspace**.
2. In the **Work email** box, type your email address. If you entered your email on the previous page, it is pre-filled.
3. Click **Continue**. The **User Authentication** page appears.

   ![Log in to a different workspace](/images/logintodiffws.gif)

   <Info>
     User Authentication uses the same 6-digit TOTP code as 2-step verification. MoEngage verifies your identity at this step — before listing your workspaces — because workspace credentials (password, Google, SSO) are workspace-specific and cannot identify you across all workspaces. For information on setting up your authenticator app, refer to [Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device](#set-up-2-step-verification-on-your-device).
   </Info>

4. Open your authenticator app, find the 6-digit code for your MoEngage account, and type it in the verification box.
5. Click **Continue**. The **Select Workspace** page appears.
6. Select the workspace and environment you want to access. Each workspace entry shows:
   - Workspace name
   - Whether it is your last-used workspace or not
   - Environment (Live or Test)

   ![Select workspace and log in](/images/logindiffws2.gif)

7. Based on the login method configured for the selected workspace, complete authentication:
   - **Password**: Enter your password and click **Login**.
   - **Google Sign-In**: Click **Continue with Google** and choose your Google account.
   - **SSO**: Click **Continue using SSO** to be redirected to your organization's Identity Provider (IdP).

You are logged in to the selected workspace.

## Log In via MCP Connection

When you initiate a MoEngage connection from an MCP (MoEngage Connection Protocol) client, you are redirected to the MoEngage's MCP authentication page. MCP connections by default allows you to select the workspace you wish to log into.

1. In your MCP client, initiate a new MoEngage connection. You are redirected to the MoEngage global login page.
2. In the **Work email** box, type your email address and click **Continue**. You are redirected to the login page for your last-used workspace's data center.
3. On the **User Authentication** page, open your authenticator app, find the 6-digit code for MoEngage, and type it in the verification box. Click **Continue**.
4. On the **Select Workspace** page, choose the workspace and environment you want to connect to.
5. Complete authentication based on the workspace's configured login method (password, Google, or SSO).

Your MCP connection is established.

<Info>
  MCP sessions last up to 30 days (expiry), with a 7-day idle timeout. MCP sessions are independent from your MoEngage Dashboard session — logging into a workspace via MCP does not create or affect an active Dashboard session, and vice versa.
  If your flow is interrupted for any reason whatsoever (password not set, 2-step verification configuration not set, network issues, etc.), you must initiate a new connection again.
</Info>

## Complete 2-Step Verification

During the last-used workspace login flow, if 2-step verification is enabled, MoEngage shows the 2-Step Verification page after you authenticate.

- **If you haven't configured 2-step verification on your device yet**, the page prompts you to set it up. For more information, refer to [Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device](#set-up-2-step-verification-on-your-device). <img src="/images/moengage_943be8.png" alt="2-step verification setup prompt" />
- **If you've already configured 2-step verification**, enter the code from your authenticator app: <img src="/images/moengage_7fbf4b.png" alt="2-step verification code entry" />
  1. Open the authenticator app on your device and find the 6-digit code for your MoEngage workspace.
  2. On the 2-Step Verification page, type the code in the **Verification code** boxes.
  3. Click **Verify**. You are logged in to the MoEngage dashboard.

<Tip>
  If you cannot complete 2-step verification — for example, you've lost your device or run out of recovery codes — see [Unable to Log In](#unable-to-log-in).
</Tip>

<Info>
  In the [Log Into a Different Workspace](#log-into-a-different-workspace) flow, the equivalent step is called **User Authentication** and happens before workspace selection, not after.
</Info>

## Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device

When 2-step verification is required, you must complete a one-time setup on your device using an authenticator app. After setup, your authenticator app generates a 6-digit code you'll use each time you log in.

<Tip>
  **Prerequisites**

  You must have an authenticator app on your phone (for example, Google Authenticator).

  - Google Authenticator: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.authenticator2&hl=en_IN&gl=US) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-authenticator/id388497605)
  - Twilio Authy: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.authy.authy) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/twilio-authy/id494168017)
</Tip>

### When You're Prompted to Set Up 2-Step Verification

You'll be prompted to set up 2-step verification in these scenarios:

- Your admin signs up for MoEngage or creates a new workspace. MoEngage enables 2-step verification by default.
- You're invited to a workspace that has 2-step verification enabled. After [finishing your workspace setup](/user-guide/getting-started/initial-setup/sign-up-with-moengage-or-create-a-new-account-in-moengage#finish-your-workspace-setup-as-an-invited-user), MoEngage prompts you to set up 2-step verification.
- Your admin enables 2-step verification after you've already logged in, and you're automatically logged out. You're prompted on your next login.
- You enable 2-step verification for your own workspace in **My Profile**.

In each scenario you land on the 2-Step Verification page:

### Troubleshoot 2-Step Verification Issues

If you're having trouble with 2-step verification:

- Make sure both your login device and the device with your authenticator app are set to the same timezone. Codes are time-based and expire after 30 seconds.
- Clear your browser's cookies and cache.
- Verify you're using the correct login URL for your workspace. Each workspace is hosted on a specific data center. For more information, refer to [Data Centers in MoEngage](/user-guide/data/key-concepts/data-centers-in-moengage).
- If you don't receive the reset email, check that your organization hasn't blocked the MoEngage domain.

If the issue persists, [contact the MoEngage Support team](/user-guide/contact-support/raise-a-support-ticket-through-moengage-dashboard) or your Customer Success Manager (CSM).

## Recovery Codes

Recovery codes are a set of 10 one-time-use backup codes that let you log in to your MoEngage workspace when your primary 2-step verification device is unavailable. MoEngage generates these codes when you first set up or reset 2-step verification.

Store your recovery codes securely and do not share them. Each code can only be used once—once used, MoEngage marks it as invalid.

If you use all your recovery codes, [reset 2-step verification](#reset-2-step-verification) to generate a new set.

- You cannot authenticate using the verification code from your current authenticator app (for example, you lost or replaced your device).

## Unable to Log In

### Forgot Your Password

If you forget your password, you can reset it from the login page.

1. Go to your MoEngage workspace login page.
2. Click **Reset password**.
   ![Forgot password link](/images/moengage_d26a75.png)
   The Reset password page appears.
3. In the **Email** box, type your MoEngage email address.
4. Click **Send Link**.
   ![Send reset link](/images/moengage_74e4cd.png)
   MoEngage sends a password reset link to your email.
5. Open the email from MoEngage and click the reset link.
   ![Password reset email](/images/moengage_d301e8.png)
6. On the **Reset password** page:
   1. In the **New password** box, type your new password. The password must:
      - Contain 8 to 20 characters.
      - Contain at least one uppercase letter.
      - Contain at least one number.
      - Contain at least one special character from: `! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) _ ? / , < > ; : { } | ~` with no spaces.
   2. In the **Confirm password** box, retype the same password.
      <Info>
        Choose a password you haven't used before.
      </Info>
   3. Click **Reset Password**.
      ![Reset password form](/images/moengage_8b2aef.png)

MoEngage resets your password and redirects you to the login page. [Log in](#log-in-to-your-last-used-workspace) using your new password.

### Lost or Replaced Your 2-Step Verification Device

If you no longer have access to the device with your authenticator app, [reset 2-step verification](#reset-2-step-verification) to reconfigure it on a new device.

### Log In Using a Recovery Code

If your primary 2-step verification device is temporarily unavailable, use one of your downloaded recovery codes to log in.

<Tip>
  **Prerequisites**

  You must have already downloaded your recovery codes. Recovery codes are available when you first set up or reset 2-step verification. For more information, refer to [Recovery Codes](#recovery-codes).
</Tip>

1. [Log in to your workspace](#log-in-to-your-last-used-workspace) using your credentials.
2. On the 2-Step Verification page, click **Enter a 2-step recovery code**. <img src="/images/moengage_afb7a9.png" alt="Enter a 2-step recovery code link" /> The 2-Step Verification page refreshes.
3. In the **Recovery code** box, type one of your saved recovery codes.
   <Info>
     Each recovery code is unique and can only be used once. Once used, MoEngage marks it as invalid.
   </Info>
4. Click **Verify**. <img src="/images/moengage_436916.png" alt="Verify recovery code" /> You are logged in to the MoEngage dashboard.

<Tip>
  If you've used all of your recovery codes, [reset 2-step verification](#reset-2-step-verification) to generate a new set.
</Tip>
