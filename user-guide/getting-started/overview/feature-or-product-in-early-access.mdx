The Early Access tag on a product or feature indicates that the product or feature is not yet generally available and is offered as an early-access release.

# What is Early Access?

MoEngage offers early access when a product or feature is functionally complete and development is done, but the feature may still have some bugs or performance issues. Early access is intended for users who want to test the product, use it cautiously, and report issues.

# What Does Early Access Mean for MoEngage Users?

If you are seeing an early-access product or feature, you are among the users who either requested the feature or whom the MoEngage Product team considered a relevant fit for it.

Use any early-access feature cautiously, as it might have low-impact bugs that escaped testing (QA). Reporting any bugs or performance issues you encounter helps MoEngage improve your experience and deliver a stable product.

For further assistance, please contact your MoEngage Customer Success Manager (CSM) or the Support team.
