description: "Understand what early access means in MoEngage, what to expect from early-access features, and which features are currently in early access."
---

MoEngage offers a feature in early access when it is functionally complete but still maturing, and it may have minor bugs or performance issues. If you see an early-access feature in your dashboard, you are among the users who either requested it or whom the MoEngage Product team considered a good fit for it.

Use early-access features cautiously, as they might have low-impact issues that escaped testing (QA). Reporting any bugs or performance issues you encounter helps MoEngage improve the feature and deliver a stable experience. To request access to any of the features below, contact your MoEngage Customer Success Manager (CSM) or the Support team.
