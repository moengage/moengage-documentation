This page describes what you need before you start, how to install the app, and how to sign in for the first time.

## Prerequisites

To use the app, you need:

- An existing MoEngage account with access to the web dashboard. The app is for users who already use MoEngage on the web; you sign in with the same account and workspace. There is no separate sign-up in the app.
- Permission to view the campaigns you want to monitor. The app shows the same campaigns and data your account can access on the web.
- If 2-step verification is enabled for your account, it must be set up on your device. See [Set Up 2-Step Verification on Your Device](/user-guide/getting-started/initial-setup/set-up-2-step-verification-on-your-device).
- If you approve campaigns, the campaign approval flow must be enabled for your team on the web dashboard.

## Install the app

The app is available for iOS and Android.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your app store">
    On your device, open the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the app">
    Search for the MoEngage mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install and open">
    Install the app and open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in

You can sign in to the app with your existing MoEngage account, use the same workspace and login method you use on the web dashboard. The methods available to you depend on the login method configured for your workspace: password, Google, or SSO.

  <Tab title="Sign in with SSO">

1. On the login screen, in the **Work email** box, type your email address.
2. Sign in with your workspace's method
    - **Password**: type your password and tap **Login**.
    - **Google**: tap **Sign in with Google** and choose your Google account.
    - **SSO**: tap **Login using SSO**, type your work email on the **Login with Single Sign On** page, tap **Login**, and authenticate with your organization's identity provider.

  If you have forgotten your password, use **Forgot password?** to reset it.

3. If [2-step verification](/user-guide/getting-started/initial-setup/set-up-2-step-verification-on-your-device) is enabled for your account, you can choose between an Authenticator app OTP or an email OTP (note that the email OTP process includes a timer, while the Authenticator app process does not). Retrieve the 6-digit code from your preferred method, type it in the **Verification code** boxes, and tap **Verify**. The first time, you are prompted to set up 2-step verification on your device.
4. After you sign in, the app opens to the **Campaigns** screen for your default workspace.

## Enable push notifications

To receive notifications on your device, allow notifications when the app prompts you. If you skip this step, you can enable notifications later in your device settings for the app. Without notifications enabled, they still appear in the Notifications tab, but you will not be notified when they arrive.
