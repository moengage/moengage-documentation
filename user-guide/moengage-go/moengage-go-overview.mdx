The MoEngage mobile app (MoEngage Go) lets you monitor campaign performance, manage running campaigns, and approve campaigns pending review from your phone. It is a companion to the MoEngage web dashboard, focused on the tasks you need to complete when you are away from your desk.
 
<Info>The app is available for iOS and Android.</Info>

## What the app does
 
The mobile app gives you read access to your campaign data and a focused set of actions you can perform on the go. It does not replace the web dashboard; instead, it surfaces the information and controls most often needed outside of a working session at a desk. The app is organized into three main tabs accessible from the bottom navigation bar, along with detailed drill-down screens: 

- **Campaigns**: The default landing screen where you can monitor and manage your campaigns. At the top of this screen, you can switch workspaces using the dropdown. You can toggle between All Campaigns and My Campaigns, and use the search bar or filter icon to locate specific campaigns. Each campaign is displayed as a card summarizing its status, channel, timestamps, and high-level performance metrics (such as Sent, Open, and Clicks).
- **Notifications**: Access your alerts, delivery failures, and pending approval requests.
- **Profile**: Access your account details, app version, help resources, and sign-out options.
- **Campaign Overview**: Tapping on any individual campaign card from the Campaigns tab opens its detail screen. It shows the campaign's key information, analytics and info. 

## Intended Audience
 
The app is intended for marketers, campaign managers, and product owners who need to:
 
- Check how campaigns are performing without opening the web dashboard.
- Take immediate action on a campaign, such as pausing or stopping it.
- Approve or reject campaigns submitted by their team.
- Stay informed about campaign failures, expiries, and significant changes in key metrics.

|---|---|---|
| View active, scheduled, and recent campaigns | Yes | Yes |
| Switch between workspaces | Yes | Yes |
| Search and filter campaigns | Yes | Yes |
| View campaign details, creative, and audience | Yes | Yes |

| Pause or stop an active campaign | Yes | Yes |

| Create or edit campaigns | No | Yes |

| Flows, Inform, Personalize, and Recommendations | No | Yes |
 

<Info>
    - The app reads from the same campaign data as the web dashboard, so the campaigns, statistics, and audiences you see in the app match what you see on the web.
    - The app supports a limited set of campaign actions. To create a campaign, edit campaign content, or build a segment, use the web dashboard.
    - Performance statistics in the app are high-level. For detailed analytics, breakdowns, and custom reports, use the web dashboard.
    - Alert types and thresholds are configured on the web dashboard. The app displays the alerts you have already set up there.
</Info>

## Use cases
 
The following use cases describe the tasks the app is designed to support.
 
 - **Monitor campaign performance away from your desk**: When you are in a meeting, commuting, or otherwise away from the web dashboard, open the Home screen to see how your campaigns are performing. The real-time performance snapshot aggregates results across all campaigns in the selected workspace for the current day, and the active and scheduled campaign counts tell you what is currently live. To review a specific campaign, open its Campaign Overview screen and select a time period (1 day, 7 days, 30 days, or a custom range) to see impressions, clicks, and conversions.
 - **Pause or stop a campaign immediately**: If a live campaign is underperforming, sending to the wrong audience, or affected by an external event, you can act without waiting until you are back at your desk. Open the campaign from the Campaigns tab or the Home screen, review its details and current performance, and pause or stop it from the Campaign Overview screen.
 - **Approve or reject campaigns submitted by your team**: If campaign approval is enabled for your team, campaigns awaiting your review appear in the Notifications tab and in **My campaigns** on the Home screen. You can review a campaign's details and creative on your phone and approve or reject it, so campaign work is not blocked while you are away from the dashboard.
 - **Look up campaign details and audience**: When you need to confirm what a campaign contains or who it targets, use search and filters in the Campaigns tab to find it, then open the Campaign Overview screen. There you can view the campaign's key information, a preview of the creative, and the segment or audience it is sent to.
