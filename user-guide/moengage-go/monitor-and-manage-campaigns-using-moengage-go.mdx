After logged-in, you are taken to the **Campaigns** screen in the MoEngage mobile app (EngageBuddy). In this screen you can monitor campaign performance, find and organize campaigns, and act on them from your phone. This page explains how to navigate the app, find campaigns, read campaign cards, and take action.

## View your Workspace and Navigation

- Before you review campaigns, make sure you are viewing the correct workspace. At the top of the screen, click the dropdown to switch between workspaces in your organization. A status dot indicates the active workspace. The campaign list reflects the selected workspace.

The navigation bar at the bottom of the screen is available throughout the app:

- **Campaigns**: Monitor active, scheduled, and past campaigns.
- **Notifications**: Respond to alerts, delivery failures, and pending approval requests.
- **Profile**: Access your account settings and preferences, switch workspaces, and sign out.

## Find and Organize Campaigns

Use the views, search, sort, and filter controls to locate campaigns.

### View Campaign

- **All campaigns**: Every campaign in the selected workspace, regardless of who created it.
- **My campaigns**: Only the campaigns you created or are assigned to manage.

### Search, Sort, and Filter

- **Search**: Type a campaign name or ID to jump directly to it.
- **Sort**:  Reorder the list by creation date, performance, or name.
- **Filter**: Narrow the list by parameters such as channel (for example, Push, Email), platform (for example, Android, iOS), and status.

## Monitor Campaigns

Each campaign appears as a card that summarizes its configuration and performance.

### Configuration

- **Campaign name**: The campaign's title, for example `Christmas apparel offers`.
- **Channel and platform**: How and where the message is delivered, for example Push (One time) on Android, or SMS (Periodic) on iOS.
- **Timestamps**:  When the campaign was created (**Created on**) and, if applicable, sent (**Sent on**).

### Status

A color-coded badge shows the campaign's current stage:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | Currently running. |
| Scheduled | Configured and awaiting its launch time. |
| Sent | A one-time campaign that has finished sending. |
| Expired | A campaign that has passed its end date. |

Other statuses, such as Paused, Stopped, Under review, Rejected, and Draft, can also appear.

**Performance metrics**

For campaigns that are active or have concluded, the card shows engagement metrics:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Impressions | The number of times the campaign message was successfully displayed to users. |
| Clicks | The number of users who tapped, clicked, or interacted with the campaign message. |
| Conversions | The number of users who completed the designated goal or event after engaging with the campaign. |
| Failed | The number of messages that could not be delivered because of technical errors, opt-outs, or invalid contact details. |

Each metric also shows a horizontal bar and a percentage. These represent the percentage breakdown for the metric and help you compare metrics and identify the drop-off between impressions, clicks, and conversions.

## Campaign Actions

The action menu (the three dots on the right of each card) opens the actions available for that campaign. The available actions depend on your user role and the campaign's status, and can include:

- **Manage the campaign** — Pause an active campaign or duplicate a campaign. Editing campaign content opens in your browser, because the campaign builder is not optimized for mobile.
- **Approve or reject** — If your organization uses approval workflows, review pending campaigns and approve or reject them from this menu or from the Notifications tab.
