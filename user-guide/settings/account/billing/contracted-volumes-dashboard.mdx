## Overview

The Contracted Volumes Dashboard provides real-time visibility into how your actual usage compares to your contracted slabs (service tiers). Monitor your consumption across all billing metrics—Emails, SMS, WhatsApp, push, and more—and see exactly where you stand throughout your billing cycle.

## Access the Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Billing">
    In the MoEngage dashboard, click **Settings** > **Account** > **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Usage Tab">
    Click the **Usage** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Contracted Volumes View">
    In the filters at the top:
    - Set **Select workspace(s)** to **Account** (not individual workspaces).
    - Set **Date** to **Current subscription period**.
    
    The dashboard automatically switches to show your contracted volumes and consumed data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dashboard Layout

The Contracted Volumes Dashboard displays:

- **Metrics**: Rows for each billing metric in your contract (Emails Sent, SMS Sent, WhatsApp Messages, MAU, etc.).
- **Columns**: Each column represents a month within your billing cycle, plus a **Total** column (shown only when viewing multiple months).
- **Progress Bars**: Each cell shows a color-coded progress bar:
  - <Badge>Green</Badge>: Below 80% of your base slab.
  - <Badge>Amber</Badge>: 80–99% of your base slab.
  - <Badge>Red</Badge>: 100%+ of your base slab (overage).
- **Slab Information**: Displays your base slab, Slab 2, Slab 3 (if applicable), and overage rates for each metric.

## Filter & Customize Your View

Use the filters at the top to focus on the data that matters:

### Select Workspace(s)
- **Account**: View aggregated data across all workspaces in your billing account (requires **Manage account details** permission).
- **Workspaces**: Select one or more specific workspaces. Only workspaces attached to your billing account are visible.

<Info>
  When you select individual workspaces instead of Account, the contracted volumes view is hidden and the dashboard switches to the standard usage trends view.
</Info>

### Date
- **Current subscription period** (default): Shows all months from your subscription start date to the present.
- **Month names**: Select a specific month to focus on that month's consumption.
- **Last 3/6/12 months**: Preset ranges for quick comparison.

### Select Month (Optional)
Narrow the view to a specific month within your subscription period. Date ranges update to reflect your subscription start and end dates.

<Info>
  If a month spans two subscription periods, consumption is prorated based on the number of days in that month for each period.
</Info>

## Understanding the Data

### Color Coding

Each consumed volume cell shows a progress bar with color indicators:

| Color | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Badge>Green</Badge> | Below 80% of base slab | On track. Continue monitoring. |
| <Badge>Amber</Badge> | 80–99% of base slab | Approaching limit. Plan an upgrade or reduce send volume. |
| <Badge>Red</Badge> | 100%+ of base slab | Exceeded contracted limit. Incurring overage charges. |

### Metric Name Highlighting

If any month for a metric shows red (overage), the metric name itself appears in red, helping you spot which channels are running over budget.

### Total Column

When viewing multiple months, the rightmost **Total** column shows aggregate consumption across all selected months, helping you see overall usage for the billing cycle.

### Slabs Explained

For each metric, the dashboard shows:

- **Base Slab**: Your primary contracted volume.
- **Slab 2** (if applicable): A higher tier available for mid-cycle upgrades.
- **Slab 3** (if applicable): An additional tier.
- **Overage**: The per-unit cost if you exceed all slabs.

If a tier doesn't apply to your contract, it displays as "–".

## Track Trends & Forecast

Watch for patterns month-to-month:

- **Steady growth**: Usage gradually increasing (typical during ramp-up).
- **Sudden spikes**: Unexpected jumps (indicate campaign launches or data quality issues).
- **Approaching limits**: Metrics trending toward amber or red—plan upgrades before month-end.

Use this insight to forecast usage for your next renewal.

## Download Your Data

Export your contracted volumes as CSV for reporting and forecasting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Download CSV">
    At the top right of the table, click **Download CSV**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the File">
    Open in Excel, Google Sheets, or your data tool. The CSV includes:
    - Your subscription period and selected month range.
    - All metrics visible in the current view.
    - Consumed volumes for each month.
    - Slab information (base, Slab 2, Slab 3, overage rates).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### CSV Contents

- Account & subscription info (account name, workspace names, Salesforce ID, subscription period).
- Monthly breakdown of consumed volumes by metric.
- Slab tiers (base slab, Slab 2, Slab 3, overage rates).
- Evaluation criteria (monthly, quarterly, or annual).
- Admin contact emails and timezone.

## Request a Slab Increase

If you're approaching or exceeding your limits:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the Metric">
    Look for the amber or red metric in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Request Slab Increase">
    A "Request Slab Increase" button appears when you're near limits. Click to notify your Account Manager immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan the Upgrade">
    Your Account Manager reaches out within one business day to discuss:
    - Your current usage trend.
    - Available upgrade options (Slab 2, Slab 3, or custom rates).
    - Pricing and contract amendment details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Alternatively, contact your Account Manager directly with the metric name and desired increase.

## Handle Overages

If you exceed your contracted limits:

1. **Identify the overage**: Red cells in the dashboard indicate months where consumption exceeded slabs.
2. **Understand the cost**: Your invoice will include charges at the overage rate.
3. **Plan an upgrade**: Use "Request Slab Increase" or contact your Account Manager to upgrade before the next cycle.
4. **Set an alert**: Configure a [billing alert](/user-guide/settings/account/billing/usage-details#create-alerts) to notify you when approaching limits in the future.

## FAQs

<Accordion title="Why is one month showing much higher consumption than others?">
  Possible reasons:
  - You launched a large campaign or promotion that month.
  - You imported historical data or backfilled user records.
  - A data quality issue caused unexpected event spikes.
  
  Check your campaign calendar or data imports. If unexpected, reach out to your Account Manager.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I upgrade my slab mid-cycle?">
  Yes. Click **Request Slab Increase** in the dashboard, or contact your Account Manager. Most upgrades are processed within one business day.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I hit 100% of my slab?">
  Your contract determines the behavior:
  - Some allow unlimited overages at a per-unit rate.
  - Others throttle or pause campaigns to prevent further overages.
  
  Check your invoice or contact your Account Manager for your specific terms.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I view year-over-year trends?">
  The dashboard shows months within your current subscription period. To compare across years, download multiple months' data and analyze in a spreadsheet. Contact your Account Manager for custom reports.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why am I seeing prorated consumption for a month?">
  If your subscription start or end date falls mid-month, that month's consumption is prorated (divided by the number of days in that month) so you only see the usage for your active days in that period.
</Accordion>
