## Overview

The Contracted Volumes Dashboard shows your consumption against your contracted limits. Slabs are billing tiers—different price levels in your contract. Track usage across all billing metrics—Emails Sent, SMS Sent, WhatsApp Messages, Monthly Active Users, and more—month by month throughout your billing cycle.

<Info>
  **Account-level feature:** This dashboard displays at the Account level only. Workspace views show standard Usage Trends instead. Set your workspace filter to **Account** to access Contracted Volumes.
</Info>

<Info>
  **Phased rollout:** The Contracted Volumes Dashboard is rolling out to select accounts based on contract data quality in Salesforce. If you do not see this dashboard, contact your Account Manager to check eligibility status.
</Info>

For more information on your active products and service dates, refer to [Entitlements](/user-guide/settings/account/billing/usage-details#view-entitlements).

## Who Uses This Dashboard

Different roles use this dashboard for different purposes:

- **Finance Leads & Billing Managers:** Track spending, forecast costs, manage budgets across the organization
- **Growth Managers & Campaign Managers:** Monitor channel capacity, prevent campaigns from throttling mid-send, plan send timing around usage patterns
- **Account Admins:** Oversee account health, alert team members when limits approach, request upgrades

## How Your Contract Data Works

All slab information displays from Salesforce—your single source of truth for contract details. Your Account Manager sets slabs in Salesforce based on your contract. You cannot modify slabs in MoEngage; changes happen in Salesforce and appear here automatically.

**Your slabs control:**
- When campaigns throttle or pause due to limit exhaustion
- Overage costs if you exceed contracted limits
- Which channels have capacity remaining for new campaigns

If your slabs do not match your contract or do not update when you upgrade, your Account Manager corrects them in Salesforce. For details on contract management in your source system, refer to [Salesforce Contract Management](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.contracts_overview.htm).

## Access the Dashboard

In MoEngage, navigate to **Settings** > **Account** > **Billing** > **Usage** tab. Set both filters as follows:
- **Select workspace(s)**: Account
- **Date**: Current subscription period

The dashboard displays your contracted volumes and consumed data automatically.

<Info>
  **Data source:** This dashboard pulls all slab and contract information directly from Salesforce. Changes to your contract in Salesforce appear here automatically (typically within 24 hours). If data appears incorrect or missing, your Account Manager corrects it in Salesforce as the source of truth.
</Info>

## Dashboard Components

### Metrics and Columns

Each row displays a billing metric from your contract. Columns show:

- **Monthly volumes:** Your usage each month (Jan, Feb, Mar, etc.)
- **Total column:** Sums all selected months (appears only when viewing multiple months)
- **Progress bars:** Color-coded indicators showing your usage level

### Color-Coded Progress Bars

- <Badge>Green</Badge>: Below 80% of your base slab (on track)
- <Badge>Amber</Badge>: 80–99% of your base slab (approaching limit)
- <Badge>Red</Badge>: 100%+ of your base slab (overage)

If a metric shows red in any month, the metric name highlights in red for quick visibility.

### Slab Information Column

Displays the number of price tiers (slabs) in your contract and their evaluation schedule:

**Slab tiers:**
- **Base Slab:** Your primary limit
- **Slab 2:** Higher tier available for mid-cycle upgrades (if you have it)
- **Slab 3:** Additional tier (if you have it)
- **Overage:** Cost per unit above all limits

## Understanding Billing Metrics

### How Evaluation Periods Work

Different metrics evaluate on different schedules. This changes when you get charged for overages.

Check the Slab Information column to see each metric's evaluation schedule:

| Evaluation | How It Works | When You Pay Overages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | Each month's consumption compares to the monthly slab separately | Monthly charges reset each month |
| **Annual** | All months' consumption sums and compares to the annual slab total | Annual charges only when yearly total exceeds slab |

### Real-World Example

The following comparison demonstrates how monthly and annual evaluation produce different billing outcomes:

| Month | Total MTUs (Monthly) | Open Analytics (Annual) | When You Get Billed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January | 11K of 10K = **OVER** | 5M of 25M = OK | **NOW** (Jan invoice) |
| February | 9K of 10K = OK | 11M of 25M = OK | No charge |
| March | — | 18M of 25M = OK | No charge |
| April | — | 26M of 25M = **OVER** | **NOW** (Apr invoice) |

**Why this matters:** With monthly evaluation, each month resets independently. January overages charge in January; February starts fresh. With annual evaluation, months accumulate. You only pay overages when the yearly total exceeds your slab. Understanding your metric's evaluation period helps you forecast costs and plan upgrades.

### Product Activation Dates

Products in your contract have different start dates but share the same end date:

| Product | Start | End | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total MTUs | Jan 13, 2026 | Jan 12, 2027 | Monthly |
| WhatsApp Messages Sent | Feb 1, 2026 | Jan 12, 2027 | Monthly |
| Open Analytics Usage | Apr 1, 2026 | Jan 12, 2027 | Annually |

Products show "–" for months before they start. You activate products at different times. Each product's billing starts on its activation date. All products end billing when your contract renews.

## Filter Options

### Date Filters

- **Current subscription period** (default): All months from subscription start to present
- **Previous subscription period**: Prior completed contract periods (if available)
- **Calendar filters**: "This month," "Last month," or custom ranges

<Info>
  Consumption aggregates at the **monthly level** only. Individual days do not display. If your subscription starts or ends mid-month, that month counts as a complete month for slab comparison.
</Info>

## View and Analyze Your Consumption

The dashboard includes both table and chart views. Use them to understand trends and plan upgrades.

### Table View

Identify patterns month by month:

- **Steady growth:** Gradual increases (typical during ramp-up)
- **Sudden spikes:** Unexpected jumps signal campaign launches or data issues
- **Approaching limits:** Amber or red metrics require upgrade planning before month-end

### Charts View

Charts display slab thresholds as dotted lines alongside your consumption:

- **Colored lines:** Show your consumption
- **Dotted lines:** Show slab thresholds (one per tier)
- **Red line:** Overage threshold
- **Intersection points:** Where consumption crosses a slab line indicates your move to the next pricing tier

## Export Your Data

Export contracted volumes for reporting and forecasting.

1. Click **Download CSV** at the top right of the table
2. Open in Excel, Google Sheets, or your analytics tool

**CSV includes:**
- Subscription period and selected month range
- All metrics in your current view
- Monthly consumed volumes by metric
- Slab tiers and evaluation criteria
- Account and workspace names, Salesforce ID, admin emails, timezone

## Request a Slab Increase

When approaching or exceeding limits, request an upgrade:

1. Identify the amber or red metric in the dashboard
2. Click **Request Slab Increase** (appears when near limits) to notify your Account Manager
3. Your Account Manager discusses available options (Slab 2, Slab 3, custom rates) within one business day

Alternatively, contact your Account Manager directly with the metric name and desired increase.

## Handle Overages

If you exceed your contracted limits:

1. **Identify:** Red cells show months where consumption exceeded slabs
2. **Understand cost:** Your invoice includes overage charges at the rate specified in your contract
3. **Plan upgrade:** Request a slab increase before the next cycle
4. **Monitor future periods:** Set up billing alerts to notify you when approaching limits

Refer to the [billing alerts guide](/user-guide/settings/account/billing/usage-details) for alert setup instructions.

## Impact on Your Campaigns

**For Growth Managers & Campaign Managers:**

When you approach or exceed contracted limits, your campaigns may throttle (send at reduced volume) or pause entirely. This dashboard prevents surprise send delays by showing you exactly where you stand.

**Practical scenarios:**

| Situation | What This Dashboard Prevents |
| --- | --- |
| **Planning a large campaign send** | Red Email slab indicates insufficient capacity → request upgrade before launching to ensure full volume delivery |
| **Multiple campaigns running simultaneously** | Monitor multiple channels simultaneously; SMS at 85% capacity requires reducing non-critical sends to maintain critical delivery |
| **Month-end rush** | WhatsApp slab approaching 100% with 10 days remaining requires immediate slab increase request or pause of lower-priority sends |
| **Unexpected traffic spike** | Detect spikes in real-time and contact your Account Manager for immediate upgrade before campaigns are throttled |

**Use this dashboard as your early warning system.** Monitor it to prevent campaigns from pausing mid-send, ensure critical sends complete on time, and align channel strategy with available capacity.

## Resolve Missing Slab Data

If the dashboard displays **"Slab information not available"** for a metric:

- The contracted slab data in Salesforce is missing or incomplete
- Consumption data still displays; slab comparison and color coding do not
- Contact your Account Manager to verify and correct contract details
- The dashboard updates automatically once corrected (typically within 24 hours)

This is a one-time fix. After it's corrected, all metrics display with complete slab information and color coding.

## FAQs

<Accordion title="Why does one month show much higher consumption than others?">
  Possible causes:
  - Large campaign or promotion launched that month
  - Historical data imported or user records backfilled
  - Data quality issue causing event spikes
  
  Check your campaign calendar or data imports. Contact your Account Manager if unexpected.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I upgrade my slab mid-cycle?">
  Yes. Click **Request Slab Increase** in the dashboard, or contact your Account Manager. Most upgrades process within one business day.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when I hit 100% of my slab?">
  Your contract determines the behavior. Some contracts allow unlimited overages at a per-unit rate; others throttle or pause campaigns to prevent further overages. Check your invoice or contact your Account Manager for your specific terms.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I view trends across multiple years?">
  The dashboard shows months within your current subscription period. To compare across prior years, download multiple months' data and analyze in a spreadsheet, or contact your Account Manager for custom reports.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does my month show prorated consumption?">
  If your subscription starts or ends mid-month, that month's consumption is prorated so the data reflects only your active days in that period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How often does the dashboard update?">
  The dashboard updates in near real-time as consumption occurs. Usage data displays with minimal latency as campaigns run and messages deliver.
</Accordion>
