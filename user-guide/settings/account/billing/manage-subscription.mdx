MoEngage offers simple, uncomplicated pricing to help teams of all sizes deliver the right omni-channel customer experience for your business. Click [here](https://www.moengage.com/plans-and-pricing/) to read more about the plans MoEngage has on offer.

MoEngage uses a unique Subscription Code to activate the right plan for your workspace. You can reach out to your designated customer success manager to get the code.

## Activate Your Subscription

### Option A: Welcome Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the welcome prompt">
    MoEngage displays a welcome prompt where you can enter your subscription code after you sign up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your subscription code">
    Enter your Subscription Code and click **Submit**.

    ![](/images/moengage_1db7e6.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Option B: Subscription Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Click **Settings** > **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your subscription code">
    Enter your Subscription Code and click **Submit**.

    ![](/images/moengage_7c01bf.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Option C: Setup Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Setup Guide">
    Click the **Need help** dropdown in the upper-right corner, then click **Setup Guide**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your subscription code">
    Enter your Subscription Code and click **Submit**.

    ![](/images/moengage_f54fae.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View Your Current Plan Details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Click **Settings** > **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your plan details">
    Select the **Subscription** tab (open by default). Click **Compare plans** to compare the plans MoEngage offers, or click **View details** on a specific plan to see its detailed feature list.

    ![](/images/moengage_de9e63.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View Subscribed Products

The **Subscribed products** section lists the products included in your active contract, so you can confirm your active subscriptions from the dashboard.

To view your subscribed products, click **Settings** > **Billing**, then select the **Subscription** tab. Use the **Search by product name** field to find a specific product. The table displays the following details for each contracted product:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The name of the product or add-on in your contract. |
| Start date | The date the product's subscription term begins. |
| End date | The date the product's subscription term ends. |

<Info>
  Only products marked as "active" in your contract appear in this list. If a product you expect to see is missing, contact your customer success manager.
</Info>

![The Subscribed products table showing product name, start date, and end date columns](/images/producttable.png)

### Download Subscribed Products

Click the **Download** icon to export your subscribed products list. The downloaded file includes the following columns:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Date | The date the export was generated. |
| Workspace Name | The workspace the export was generated from. |
| Account Name | The billing account associated with the workspace. |
| Product | The name of the product or add-on in your contract. |
| Subscription Start Date | The date the product's subscription term begins. |
| Subscription End Date | The date the product's subscription term ends. |

### Access Permissions

Viewing and downloading subscribed products uses the same permissions as the Usage page. For more information, refer to [Access Permissions](/user-guide/settings/account/billing/usage-details#access-permissions).

### FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is a product missing from my Subscribed products list?">
    Only products marked as active in your contract appear in this list. If you believe a product is missing, contact your customer success manager to confirm your contract terms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the Subscribed products list reflect what I'm billed for?">
    The Subscribed products list shows the products and add-ons included in your active contract, along with their subscription terms. For a breakdown of your usage against billing metrics, refer to [Usage Details](/user-guide/settings/account/billing/usage-details).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when a product's subscription end date passes?">
    Contact your customer success manager before a subscription end date to discuss renewal or changes to your contract.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
