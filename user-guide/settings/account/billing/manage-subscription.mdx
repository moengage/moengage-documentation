MoEngage offers simple, uncomplicated pricing to help teams of all sizes deliver the right omni-channel customer experience for your business. Click [here](https://www.moengage.com/plans-and-pricing/) to read more about the plans MoEngage has on offer.

MoEngage uses a unique Subscription Code to activate the right plan for your workspace. You can reach out to your designated customer success manager to get the code.

# How to activate your subscription?

## Option A: Welcome Guide

**Step 1:** Users are greeted with a welcome prompt after signing up. The subscription code can be entered here.

**Step 2:** Enter Subscription Code and click on Submit.

![](/images/moengage_1db7e6.png)

## Option B: Subscription Page

**Step 1**: Click on Settings -\> Billing

**Step 2:** Enter Subscription Code and click on Submit.

![](/images/moengage_7c01bf.png)

## Option C: Setup Guide

**Step 1**: Click on the "Need help" dropdown in the top right corner and click on "Setup Guide"

**Step 2:** Enter the Subscription Code and click on Submit.

![](/images/moengage_f54fae.png)

# How to view current plan details?

**Step 1:** Click on Settings -\> Billing

**Step 2:** Select the Subscription tab (open by default). Click on the "Compare plans" button to compare different plans offered by MoEngage and the "View details" button for a specific plan to view the detailed list of features.

![](/images/moengage_de9e63.png)

# View Subscribed Products

The **Subscribed products** section lists the products included in your active contract, so you can confirm your active subscriptions without contacting your account manager.

To view your subscribed products, click **Settings** > **Billing**, then select the **Subscription** tab. Use the **Search by product name** field to find a specific product. The table displays the following details for each contracted product:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The name of the product or add-on in your contract. |
| Start date | The date the product's subscription term begins. |
| End date | The date the product's subscription term ends. |

<Info>
  Only products marked as visible in your contract appear in this list. If a product you expect to see is missing, contact your customer success manager.
</Info>

![The Subscribed products table showing product name, start date, and end date columns](/images/producttable.png)

## Download Subscribed Products

Click the **Download** icon to export your subscribed products list. The downloaded file includes the following columns:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Date | The date the export was generated. |
| Workspace Name | The workspace the export was generated from. |
| Account Name | The billing account associated with the workspace. |
| Product | The name of the product or add-on in your contract. |
| Subscription Start Date | The date the product's subscription term begins. |
| Subscription End Date | The date the product's subscription term ends. |

## Access Permissions

Viewing and downloading subscribed products uses the same permissions as the Usage page. For more information, refer to [Access Permissions](/user-guide/settings/account/billing/usage-details#access-permissions).
