---
title: "Why Is My Workspace Blocked?"
description: "Resolve the 'This workspace has been blocked' error in MoEngage, typically caused by pending dues on your account."
---

## Problem

When you try to log in, you see the following error:

"This workspace has been blocked. Please contact our support team for assistance."

## Explanation

MoEngage blocks a workspace when there are pending dues on the account.

## Solution

Contact the MoEngage Support team to resolve pending dues and unblock the workspace.
